# Silencing Satchel
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*  
This magic satchel has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. As a Magic action, you can expend 1 charge and tighten the satchel’s drawstring to inflict a silencing hex on a creature you can see within 60 feet of yourself. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Charisma saving throw or be cursed for 1 minute. While the target is cursed, its mouth is sealed shut. The target can’t cast any spell with a Verbal component, consume potions, or take in food or drink. The target makes a DC 15 Charisma save at the end of each of its turns, ending the curse early on a success.
