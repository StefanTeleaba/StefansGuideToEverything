*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Crossbow Bolt Case of Plenty
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*  
This case produces an endless supply of bolts. The quiver always holds 20 *Bolts*. Another bolt is created immediately after you draw one from the case. A bolt created by the case vanishes the instant after it hits or misses a target.

*Wondrous Item, Rare*  
This case produces an endless supply of bolts. The quiver always holds 20 *+1 Bolts*. Another bolt is created immediately after you draw one from the case. A bolt created by the case vanishes the instant after it hits or misses a target.

*Wondrous Item, Very Rare*  
This case produces an endless supply of bolts. The quiver always holds 20 *+2 Bolts*. Another bolt is created immediately after you draw one from the case. A bolt created by the case vanishes the instant after it hits or misses a target.

---

A Crossbow Bolt Case of Plenty could be enchanted to supply different types of bolts: *Adamantine Bolts*, *+1 Bolts*, *Acid Bolts*, *Ranging Bolts*, etc.
