*Homebrew*
# Stone of Nightsong
*Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement)*  
You can use an action to cast one of the following spells from the Stone of Nightsong: *Ceremony*, *Gentle Repose*, *Spare the Dying*, *Speak with Dead*. The spells use your spellcasting ability and spell save DC.  
Once the stone has been used to cast a spell, it can't be used to cast that spell again until the next dawn.

---

*Critical Role: Campaign Four*  
*The stone is black and perfectly spherical, surrounded by a set of nightingale's wings.*
