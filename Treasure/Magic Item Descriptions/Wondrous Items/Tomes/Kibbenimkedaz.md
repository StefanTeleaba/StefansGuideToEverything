*Homebrew*
# Kibbenimkedaz, the Book of the Names of Stones
*Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement)*  
You can use an action to cast one of the following spells from the Kibbenimkedaz: *Legend Lore*. The spells use your spellcasting ability and spell save DC.  
Once the book has been used to cast a spell, it can't be used to cast that spell again until the next dawn.

---

*Critical Role: Campaign Four*  
*The tome is written in Druidic and has been painstakingly hand-copied. Due to its connection to the stones, the revelations obtained using this book come from the memories of the rocks themselves.*
