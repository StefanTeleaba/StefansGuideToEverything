*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Otter's Charm
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*  
You have a Swim Speed of 40 feet while wearing this amulet.

---

*Critical Role: Campaign Four*  
*The charm hangs from a leather cord. It is formed from beautiful polished "smoother than moonlight" dark basalt or volcanic rock carved to depict an otter curled up in a resting, teardrop-shaped position.*
