*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Sword of Life Stealing
*Weapon (Glaive, Greatsword, Longsword, Rapier, Scimitar, or Shortsword), Uncommon*  
When you attack a creature with this weapon and score a Critical Hit, that target takes an extra 3d6 Necrotic damage if it isn’t a Construct or an Undead, and you regain Hit Points equal to the amount of Necrotic damage dealt.

*Weapon (Glaive, Greatsword, Longsword, Rapier, Scimitar, or Shortsword), Rare*  
When you attack a creature with this weapon and score a Critical Hit, that target takes an extra 3d6 Necrotic damage if it isn’t a Construct or an Undead, and you regain Hit Points equal to the amount of Necrotic damage dealt.

*Weapon (Glaive, Greatsword, Longsword, Rapier, Scimitar, or Shortsword), Very Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.  
Any creature you hit with the weapon takes an extra 1d6 Necrotic damage if it isn’t a Construct or an Undead, and you regain Hit Points equal to the amount of Necrotic damage dealt. In addition, when you score a Critical Hit, that target takes an extra 3d6 Necrotic damage.

# Sword of Life Stealing
*Weapon (Glaive, Greatsword, Longsword, Rapier, Scimitar, or Shortsword), Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
When you attack a creature with this magic weapon and roll a 20 on the d20 for the attack roll, that target takes an extra 15 Necrotic damage if it isn’t a Construct or an Undead, and you gain Temporary Hit Points equal to the amount of Necrotic damage taken.
