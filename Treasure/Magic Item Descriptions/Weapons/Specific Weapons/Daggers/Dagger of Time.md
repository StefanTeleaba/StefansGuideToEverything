# Dagger of Time
*Weapon (Dagger), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*  
*This ornate Mithral dagger includes an hourglass at the base of the hilt which can be turned on its own axis.*  
You have a +3 Bonus to Attack and Damage rolls made with this weapon.  
***Charges.*** The dagger has 5 charges. It regains all spent charges each dawn.  
***Time Magic.*** You can spend charges to cast one of the following spells from the dagger, requiring no spell components.
1 - *Mirror Image*
2 - *Haste*
2 - *Slow* (Save DC: 19)
3 - *Revivify*
5 - *Time Stop*

*Timeturner.* As a Magic Action, you can spin the hourglass to return one creature within 60 feet of you to the state it was at the start of your previous turn; the chosen creature regains any lost Hit Points, removes any conditions, regains any spent resources and returns to its original position. If you apply this effect to yourself, the dagger is unaffected.  
Once you use the dagger in this way, you cannot do so again until the next dawn.
