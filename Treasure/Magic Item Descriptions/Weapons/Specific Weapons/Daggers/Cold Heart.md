*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Cold Heart
*Weapon (Dagger), Legendary*  
You gain a +4 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The weapon deals an extra 4d4 Acid damage on a hit.

---

*Demihuman Deities*  
*Cold Heart is a curved dagger that constantly drips acid.*  
*Cold Heart was the favored weapon of Kiaransalee, the drow goddess of undeath.*
