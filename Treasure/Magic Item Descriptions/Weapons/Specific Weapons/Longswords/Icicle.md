# Icicle
*Weapon (Longsword), Legendary*

*Icicle got its name from the sword's blade, which had turned transparent during forging.*

You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The weapon deals an extra 3d6 damage of the weapon's type if the target has Immunity or Resistance to Cold damage.  
If you take Cold damage while holding this weapon, the sword reduces the damage you take by 30. In addition, while this weapon is on your person, you and everything you wear and carry are unharmed by temperatures of 0 degrees Fahrenheit or lower.  
While wielding this weapon, you can cast *Cone of Cold* with it (save DC 18). The weapon can't cast this spell again until the next dawn.
