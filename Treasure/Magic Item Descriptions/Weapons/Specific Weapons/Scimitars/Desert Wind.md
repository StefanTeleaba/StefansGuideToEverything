# Desert Wind
*Weapon (Scimitar), Rare*

You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.  

This weapon deals an extra 1d6 Fire damage on a hit.

While holding this magic weapon, you can take a Bonus Action and use a command word to cause hot sand to swirl in a 5-foot Emanation around you. A creature moving through that area has the Blinded condition and must spend 3 feet of movement for every 1 foot it moves. The sand lasts until you take a Bonus Action to issue the command again or until you drop, stow, or sheathe the weapon.

---

*Neverwinter Nights*

This scimitar is believed to date to the period when Calimshan was ruled by genies. All of the raging heat of the desert seems to be captured within the blade of the sword. Tales of enemies surrendering after merely a scratch only seem implausible until one has tasted the blistering pain the Desert Wind causes a wound.
