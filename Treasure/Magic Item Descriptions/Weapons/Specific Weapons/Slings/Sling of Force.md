*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Sling of Force
*Weapon (Sling), Uncommon*
As long as this weapon is within your reach and you are attuned to it, your Strength score increases by 2, to a maximum of 22. In addition, you can add your Strength modifier to the damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

*Weapon (Sling), Rare*
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. In addiiton, as long as it is within your reach and you are attuned to it, your Strength score increases by 2, to a maximum of 22.  
You can add your Strength modifier to the damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

*Weapon (Sling), Very Rare*
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. In addiiton, as long as it is within your reach and you are attuned to it, your Strength score increases by 2, to a maximum of 24.  
You can add your Strength modifier to the damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

---

*A tinker without a sense of proportion, the spunky Gendlesen Barstow worried that she wasn't strong enough for the adventuring life, and so built various magical slings to enhance her strength. Although her first versions were successful, all were stolen from her workbench and eventually scattered across Faerun.*
