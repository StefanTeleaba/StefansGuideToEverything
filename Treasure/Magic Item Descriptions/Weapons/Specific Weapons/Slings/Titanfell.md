# Titanfell
*Weapon (Sling), Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. In addition, you can add your Strength modifier to the damage rolls made with this magic weapon.  
When you score a Critical Hit with this weapon, you roll all of the attack's damage dice three times instead of two times.
