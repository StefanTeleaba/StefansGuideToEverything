# Armor of Dragon Shape
*Armor (Any Light, Medium, or Heavy), Uncommon*  
When using your Wild Shape feature, you can also choose from among Dragon stat blocks.

*Armor (Any Light, Medium, or Heavy), Rare*  
You have a +1 bonus to AC while wearing this armor. When using your Wild Shape feature, you can also choose from among Dragon stat blocks.

*Armor (Any Light, Medium, or Heavy), Very Rare*  
You have a +2 bonus to AC while wearing this armor. When using your Wild Shape feature, you can also choose from among Dragon stat blocks.
