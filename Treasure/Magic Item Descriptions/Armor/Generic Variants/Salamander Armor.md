# Salamander Armor
*Armor (Any Light Armor), Uncommon*  
You have Resistance to Fire damage while you wear this armor and if a creature hits you with a melee attack roll, it takes 1d6 Fire damage.

*Armor (Any Light Armor), Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class while you wear this armor. You have Resistance to Fire damage while you wear this armor and if a creature hits you with a melee attack roll, it takes 2d6 Fire damage.

*Armor (Any Light Armor), Rare*  
You gain a +2 bonus to Armor Class while you wear this armor. You have Resistance to Fire damage while you wear this armor and if a creature hits you with a melee attack roll, it takes 3d6 Fire damage.
