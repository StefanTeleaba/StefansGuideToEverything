# Dwarven Plate
*Armor (Half Plate or Plate Armor), Uncommon*  
While wearing this armor, if an effect moves you against your will along the ground, you can reduce the distance you are moved by up to 5 feet.

*Armor (Half Plate or Plate Armor), Rare*  
While wearing this armor, you gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class. In addition, if an effect moves you against your will along the ground, you can reduce the distance you are moved by up to 10 feet.

*Armor (Half Plate or Plate Armor), Very Rare*  
While wearing this armor, you gain a +2 bonus to Armor Class. In addition, if an effect moves you against your will along the ground, you can reduce the distance you are moved by up to 20 feet.
