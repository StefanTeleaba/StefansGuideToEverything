*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Elven Chain
*Armor (Chain Mail or Chain Shirt), Uncommon*  
You are considered trained with this armor even if you lack training with Medium or Heavy Armor.

*Armor (Chain Mail or Chain Shirt), Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class while you wear this armor. You are considered trained with this armor even if you lack training with Medium or Heavy Armor.

*Armor (Chain Mail or Chain Shirt), Very Rare*  
You gain a +2 bonus to Armor Class while you wear this armor. You are considered trained with this armor even if you lack training with Medium or Heavy Armor.
