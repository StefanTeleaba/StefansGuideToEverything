# Armor of Resistance
*Armor (Any Light, Medium, or Heavy), Rare*  
You have Resistance to one type of damage while you wear this armor. The DM chooses the type or determines it randomly by rolling on the following table.

*Armor (Any Light, Medium, or Heavy), Very Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class while you wear this armor. You have Resistance to one type of damage while you wear this armor. The DM chooses the type or determines it randomly by rolling on the following table.
