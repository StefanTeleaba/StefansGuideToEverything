*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Armor of the Messenger
*Armor (Any Light Armor), Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class while you wear this armor.  
You can use an action to cast one of the following spells from the armor: *Longstrider*, *Phantom Steed*, *Sending*. The spells use your spellcasting ability and spell save DC.  
Once the armor has been used to cast a spell, it can't be used to cast that spell again until the next dawn.
