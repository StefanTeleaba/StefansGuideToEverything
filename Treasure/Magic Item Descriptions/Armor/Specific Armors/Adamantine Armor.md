*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Adamantine Armor
*Generic Variant, Uncommon*  
This suit of armor is reinforced with adamantine, one of the hardest substances in existence. While you're wearing it, any Critical Hit against you becomes a normal hit.

*Generic Variant, Rare*  
This suit of armor is reinforced with adamantine, one of the hardest substances in existence. While you're wearing it, any Critical Hit against you becomes a normal hit. In addition, when you're hit by an attack, any Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage dealt to you by that attack is reduced by 5.

*Generic Variant, Very Rare*  
This suit of armor is reinforced with adamantine, one of the hardest substances in existence. While you're wearing it, any Critical Hit against you becomes a normal hit. In addition, when you're hit by an attack, any Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage dealt to you by that attack is reduced by 10.
