*D&D Beyond Drops August 2026 - Errata*
# Salubrious Armor
*Armor (Plate Armor or Scale Mail), Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
While wearing this armor, you gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class. Whenever you regain Hit Points, the armor takes on a reddish tint, and this bonus increases to +2 until the end of your next turn.
