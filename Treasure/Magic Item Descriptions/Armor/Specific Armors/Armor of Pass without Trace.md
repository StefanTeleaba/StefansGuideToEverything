*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Armor of Pass without Trace
*Generic Variant, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*  
You radiate a concealing aura in a 10-foot Emanation. While in the aura, you and each creature you choose have a +10 bonus to Dexterity (Stealth) checks and leave no tracks.

*Generic Variant, Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
You have a +1 bonus to AC while wearing this armor. You radiate a concealing aura in a 20-foot Emanation. While in the aura, you and each creature you choose have a +10 bonus to Dexterity (Stealth) checks and leave no tracks.

*Generic Variant, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
You have a +2 bonus to AC while wearing this armor. You radiate a concealing aura in a 30-foot Emanation. While in the aura, you and each creature you choose have a +10 bonus to Dexterity (Stealth) checks and leave no tracks.

---

*The armor is made from black leather and fastened with ebony rivets that seem to emanate wisps of smoke.*
