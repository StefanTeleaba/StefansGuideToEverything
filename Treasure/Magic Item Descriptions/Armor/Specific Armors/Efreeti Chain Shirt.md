# Efreeti Chain Shirt
*Medium Armor (Chain Shirt), Uncommon*  
While wearing this armor, you know Primordial. In addition, you can stand on and move across molten rock as if it were solid ground.

*Medium Armor (Chain Shirt), Rare*  
While wearing this armor, you gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class, you have Resistance to Fire damage, and you know Primordial. In addition, you can stand on and move across molten rock as if it were solid ground.

*Medium Armor (Chain Shirt), Very Rare*  
While wearing this armor, you gain a +2 bonus to Armor Class, you have Immunity to Fire damage, and you know Primordial. In addition, you can stand on and move across molten rock as if it were solid ground.
