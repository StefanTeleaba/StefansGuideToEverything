# Foxguard Tunic
*Medium Armor (Hide Armor), Uncommon*  
You gain a bonus to your Dexterity (Acrobatics and Stealth) checks equal to your Wisdom modifier.

*Medium Armor (Hide Armor), Rare*  
You have a +1 bonus to AC while wearing this armor. You gain a bonus to your Dexterity (Acrobatics and Stealth) checks equal to your Wisdom modifier.

*Medium Armor (Hide Armor), Very Rare*  
You have a +2 bonus to AC while wearing this armor. You gain a bonus to your Dexterity (Acrobatics and Stealth) checks equal to your Wisdom modifier.
