*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Belt of the Monkey
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*  
***Climb Speed.*** You gain a Climb Speed equal to your Speed.  
***Tail.***  You grow a tail, which you can use to make Unarmed Strikes.

*Wondrous Item, Rare*  
***Monkey's Agility.*** While wearing this belt, your Dexterity score increases by 2, to a maximum of 22.  
***Climb Speed.*** You gain a Climb Speed equal to your Speed.  
***Tail.***  You grow a tail, which you can use to make Unarmed Strikes.
