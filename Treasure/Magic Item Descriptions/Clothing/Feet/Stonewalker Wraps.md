# Stonewalker Wraps
*Wondrous Item, Very Rare*

These dusty leather footwraps are stitched with a geographic pattern. While wearing them, you gain the following benefits.  
***Step of Stone.*** You gain Tremorsense with a range of 15 feet. When you take the Dash action, the range of this tremorsense increases to 30 feet until the start of your next turn.  
***Stone Blow.*** When you make an Unarmed Strike, you can replace it with a special attack. This attack summons an earthen slab from the ground below a target that you can see within 60 feet of you, striking the target with unyielding force before returning to the ground. Treat the attack as if it were a normal Unarmed Strike, except it ignores Half and Three-Quarters Cover. If you use Stunning Strike on an Unarmed Strike made in this way, the attack also deals an extra 1d6 Bludgeoning damage.  
***Stone Defense.*** When you take the Disengage or Dodge action, you can also gain Resistance to Piercing and Slashing damage until the start of your next turn.

# Stonewalker Wraps
*Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement by a Monk)*

*Stone does not bend. It does not give. It is unyielding, like you must be.*

These dusty leather footwraps are stitched with a geographic pattern. While wearing them, you gain the following benefits.  
***Step of Stone.*** You gain tremorsense out to a range of 15 feet. When you use your Step of the Wind as a bonus action, the range of this tremorsense increases to 30 feet until the start of your next turn.  
***Stone Blow.*** When you use your Flurry of Blows as a bonus action, you can replace any of the unarmed strikes you make as part of it with a special attack. This attack summons an earthen slab from the ground below a target that you can see within 60 feet of you, striking the target with unyielding force before returning to the ground. Treat the attack as if it were a normal unarmed strike, except it ignores half and three-quarters cover. If you use your Stunning Strike on an unarmed strike made in this way, the attack also deals an extra 1d6 bludgeoning damage.  
***Stone Defense.*** When you use your Patient Defense as a bonus action, you can choose to grant yourself resistance to piercing and slashing damage until the start of your next turn, instead of its normal effects.
