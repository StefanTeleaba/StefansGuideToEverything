*Drow of the Underdark, p.101*
# Trackless Boots
**Price:** 1,000 GP  
**Body Slot:** Feet  
**Aura:** Faint Transmutation  
**Associated Spell:** -  
**Weight:** -  
*These boots look rather ordinary, though of fine make.*  
***Trackless Boots.*** While you wear trackless boots, Survival checks made to follow your trail have a –5 circumstance penalty. In addition, creatures with scent cannot use this ability to detect you.
