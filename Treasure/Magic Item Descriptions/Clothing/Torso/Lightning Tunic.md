*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Lightning Tunic
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*  
If a creature hits you with a melee attack roll, the creature takes 1d8 Lightning damage.

*Wondrous Item, Rare*  
You have Resistance to Lightning damage while wearing this tunic. In addition, if a creature hits you with a melee attack roll, the creature takes 2d8 Lightning damage.

*Wondrous Item, Very Rare*  
You have Immunity to Lightning damage while wearing this tunic. In addition, if a creature hits you with a melee attack roll, the creature takes 3d8 Lightning damage.
