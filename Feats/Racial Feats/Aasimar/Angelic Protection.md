*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Angelic Protection
*Racial Feat (Prerequisite: Aasimar, Level 4+)*  
The innate resistance to magic that angels possess extends to you. You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Angelic Protection.*** You have Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
