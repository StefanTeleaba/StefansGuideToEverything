*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Mountain's Endurance
*Racial Feat (Prerequisite: Goliath: Stone Giant Ancestry, Level 4+)*  
Your ability to shrug off harm improves. You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Constitution by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Mountain's Endurance.*** When you use your *Stone's Endurance* trait, you also gain Resistance to that damage and any other damage of the same type until the start of your next turn. The Resistance is applied before *Stone's Endurance*.
