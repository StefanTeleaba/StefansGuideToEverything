*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Improved Critical Range
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Improved Critical Feature)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Improved Critical Range.*** The Critical Range for your attack rolls with weapons and Unarmed Strikes increases by 1.  
***Enhanced Critical.*** When you score a Critical Hit, you can roll one additional damage die when determining the extra damage the target takes.  
***Repeatable.*** You can take this feat more than once.
