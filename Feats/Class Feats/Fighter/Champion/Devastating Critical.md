*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Devastating Critical
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 16+, Superior Critical Feature)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Devastating Critical.*** When you score a Critical Hit, you can deal extra damage to the target equal to your Fighter level. The extra damage's type is the same as the attack's type.
