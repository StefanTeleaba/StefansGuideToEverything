*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Improved Second Wind
*Fighter Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Second Wind Feature)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength, Dexterity or Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Extra Second Wind.*** You gain 1 more use of your Second Wind feature and you regain one more expended use when you finish a Short Rest.  
***Improved Second Wind.*** When you use your Second Wind feature, you can add your Fighter Level one more time to the number of Hit Points you regain.  
***Repeatable.*** You can take this feat more than once.
