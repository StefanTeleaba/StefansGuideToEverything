*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Improved Sneak Attack
*Rogue Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Sneak Attack Feature)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Improved Sneak Attack.*** The extra damage of your Sneak Attack feature increases by one die and the size of the dice increases by one zise (d6, d8, d10, to a maximum of d12).  
***Repeatable.*** You can take this feat more than once.
