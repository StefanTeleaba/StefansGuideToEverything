*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Improved Rage
*Barbarian Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Rage Feature)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Improved Rage Damage.*** Your Rage Damage bonus increases by 1.  
***Extra Rage.*** The number of times you can enter your Rage increases by 1 and you regain one more expended use when you finish a Short Rest.  
***Repeatable.*** You can take this feat more than once.
