*D&D Beyond Drops: November 2026*
# Upper Planes Pact
*Planar Pact Feat (Prerequisite: Can’t Have Another Planar Pact Feat)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Celestial Lore.*** You gain Proficiency in the Religion skill.  
***Celestial Resistance.*** You have Resistance to Radiant damage.  
***Detect Evil and Good.*** You always have the *Detect Evil and Good* spell prepared. You can cast it once without expending a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a Short or Long Rest. You can also cast the spell using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this spell (choose when you select this feat).
