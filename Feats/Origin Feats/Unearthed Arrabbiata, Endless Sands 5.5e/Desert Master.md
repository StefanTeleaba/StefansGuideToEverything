# Desert Master
*General Feat (Prerequisites: Level 8+, Desert Warrior Feat)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Improvement.*** Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Dune Survivor.*** You have Advantage on Wisdom (Survival) and Wisdom (Animal Handling) checks made when dealing with wasteland environments or creatures native to those environments.  
***Wasteland Tactics.*** Once per turn when you hit a creature with a melee attack that is Huge sized or smaller, you can expend 15 feet of your movement to attempt and use a wasteland tactic on it. The creature must succeed a Dexterity saving throw (DC equal to 8 plus the attack’s attack modifier) or else the following effect occurs.
- The creature is knocked prone, and the ground below it becomes a 5 foot by 5 foot area of difficult terrain. In addition, the creature must expend their full movement speed to stand up from the Prone condition.
- The Creature is blinded till either the end of their next turn or until they use a Bonus Action to clear their eyes.
