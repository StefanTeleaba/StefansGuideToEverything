# Desertborn
*Origin Feat*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Heat Adapted.*** You have resistance to Fire Damage, and you have Advantage on ability checks and saving throws to avoid gaining Exhaustion levels.  
***Sandwalk.*** Your movement speed increases by 5 feet, and your movement is unaffected by difficult terrain caused by sand, soil, or loose earth. In addition, so long as you do not move more than half your movement speed on a turn, creatures are unable to detect your location using Tremorsense.
