# Desert Warrior
*General Feat General Feat (Prerequisites: Level 4+, Desertborn Feat, Strength or Dexterity 13+)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Improvement.*** Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Resilient Footing.*** You have Advantage on saving throws to avoid being pushed or knocked proneIn addition, if you would be pushed 10 or more feet away, you decrease the distance you are pushed by 5 feet.  
***Careful Strikes.*** Once per turn, You can expend 15 feet of movement to gain advantage on the next melee attack you make till the start of your next turn.  
***SandDune’s Blessing.*** If you spend at least 1 hour within an area of Extreme Heat and Bright Light, you gain a number of Temporary hit points equal to half your level.
