# Arcane Safeguard
*Origin Feat*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Cantrip.*** You learn the *Resistance* cantrip. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this spell (choose when you select this feat).  
You can cast the *Resistance* cantrip as a Bonus Action. You can do so a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.  
***Sheltering Aid.*** When you take the Help action to assist with an ally's ability check, that ally gains a number of Temporary Hit Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus.
