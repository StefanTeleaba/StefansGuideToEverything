*Northlands Worldbook 5.5e*
# Brewer
*Origin Feat*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Tool Proficiency.*** You gain proficiency in brewer's supplies.  
***Discount.*** You get a 50% discount when purchasing food and beverages.  
***Quick Brewing.*** When you finish a Long Rest, you can brew an invigorating mead, provided you have Brewer's Supplies available. A creature can use a Utilize action to drink the mead, granting it Temporary Hit Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus. The mead's potency lasts until you start a Long Rest.
