*Northlands Worldbook 5.5e*
# Sea Wolf
*Origin Feat*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Tactical Boarder.*** You have Advantage on Strength (Athletics) and Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks made to board vehicles.  
***Reaver's Ferocity.*** When you score a Critical Hit against a creature with a melee attack, you can make another melee attack against the same creature or an adjacent creature as a Bonus Action.
