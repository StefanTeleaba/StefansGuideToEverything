# Acrobat
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Strength or Dexterity 13+)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Acrobatic.*** Choose one of the following skills: Athletics or Acrobatics. If you lack proficiency with the chosen skill, you gain proficiency in it, and if you already have proficiency in it, you gain Expertise in it.
***Dash over Difficult Terrain.*** When you take the Dash action on your turn, Difficult Terrain doesn't cost you extra movement for the rest of that turn.  
***Quick Feet.*** You can take the Dash action as a Bonus Action.

*Unearthed Arcana 38: Feats for Skills*
# Acrobat
You become more nimble, gaining the following benefits:  
- Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You gain proficiency in the Acrobatics skill. If you are already proficient in the skill, you add double your proficiency bonus to checks you make with it.
- As a bonus action, you can make a DC 15 Dexterity (Acrobatics) check. If you succeed, difficult terrain doesn’t cost you extra movement until the end of the current turn.
