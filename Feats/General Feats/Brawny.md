*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Brawny
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Strength 13+)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Brawny.*** You gain proficiency in the Athletics skill. If you already have proficiency in it, you gain Expertise in it.  
***Powerful Build.*** You have Advantage on any ability check you make to end the Grappled condition. You also count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity.

*Unearthed Arcana 38: Feats for Skills*
# Brawny
You become stronger, gaining the following benefits:
- Increase your Strength score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You gain proficiency in the Athletics skill. If you are already proficient in the skill, you add double your proficiency bonus to checks you make with it.
- You count as if you were one size larger for the purpose of determining your carrying capacity.
