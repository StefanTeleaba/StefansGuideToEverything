*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Tandem Tactician
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma 13+)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Distracting Strike.*** Once per turn, when you hit a creature with an attack roll, you can distract the target. The next attack roll against the target by an attacker other than you has Advantage if the attack is made before the start of your next turn.  
***Tactician.*** You can take the Help action as a Bonus Action. In addition, you can distract enemies within 30 feet of yourself who can see or hear you.

*Unearthed Arcana 73: Feats*
# Tandem Tactician
Your presence in a scrap tends to elevate your comrades. You gain the following benefits:
- You can use the Help action as a bonus action.
- When you use the Help action to aid an ally in attacking a creature, increase the range of the Help action by 10 feet. Additionally, you can help two allies targeting the same creature within range when you use the Help action this way.
