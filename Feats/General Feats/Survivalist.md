*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Survivalist
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Wisdom 13+)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Keen Survivor.*** You always know which way is north, the number of hours left before the next sunrise or sunset, and can accurately recall anything you have seen or heard within the past month.
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Wisdom score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Survival Initiate.*** You learn one cantrip of your choice from the Druid spell list. You can cast *Longstrider* without expending a spell slot. Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells.  
***Survivalist.*** You gain proficiency in the Survival skill. If you already have proficiency in it, you gain Expertise in it.

*Unearthed Arcana 38: Feats for Skills*
# Survivalist
You master wilderness lore, gaining the following benefits:
- Increase your Wisdom score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You gain proficiency in the Survival skill. If you are already proficient in the skill, you add double your proficiency bonus to checks you make with it.
- You learn the Alarm spell. You can cast it once without expending a spell slot, and you regain the ability to do so when you finish a long rest.
