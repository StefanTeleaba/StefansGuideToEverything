*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Arcanist
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Intelligence 13+)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Intelligence score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Arcane Initiate.*** You learn one cantrip of your choice from the Wizard spell list. You can cast *Detect Magic* without expending a spell slot. Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells.  
***Arcanist.*** You gain proficiency in the Arcana skill. If you already have proficiency in it, you gain Expertise in it.

*Unearthed Arcana 38: Feats for Skills*
# Arcanist
You study the arcane arts, gaining the following benefits:
- Increase your Intelligence score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You gain proficiency in the Arcana skill. If you are already proficient in the skill, you add double your proficiency bonus to checks you make with it.
- You learn the Prestidigitation and Detect Magic spells. You can cast Detect Magic once without expending a spell slot, and you regain the ability to do so when you finish a long rest.
