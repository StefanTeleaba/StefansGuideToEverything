*Northlands Worldbook 5.5e*
# Axe Fighter
*General Feat (Prerequisites: Level 4+, Strength 13+, Martial Weapon Proficiency)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Axe Flurry.*** Once on each of your turns, if you hit a creature with a Handaxe, you can make an extra attack with a Handaxe as a Bonus Action.  
***Precise Topple.*** When you hit a creature with a Battleaxe, that creature has Disadvantage on the Constitution saving throw to avoid having the Prone condition caused by the Topple property of the Battleaxe.  
***Unending Cleave.*** The number of additional creatures you can attack using the Cleave property of a Greataxe increases by a number equal to half your Proficiency Bonus (rounded down).
