*Northlands Worldbook 5.5e*
# Blood of the Berserker
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*  
You inherited a touch of the ferocity of a berserker ancestor. You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Constitution by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Berserker Fury.*** When you are Bloodied, you can use a Bonus Action to enter a frenzied state. While in this fury, your melee weapon attacks deal extra damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you don't have the Unconscious condition when you are reduced to 0 HP (though you still make Death Saves as normal).  
Your Berserker Fury lasts for 1 minute or until you end it (no action required), or if you have the Incapacitated condition. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest.
