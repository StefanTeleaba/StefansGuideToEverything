*Northlands Worldbook 5.5e*
# Chosen by Fate
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*  
The Norns have chosen you for greater things. As a result, you gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Heroic Resurgence.*** If you use Heroic Inspiration and the die roll fails, your use of Heroic Inspiration isn't expended (though you can't use it again for the same d20 Test).  
***Heroic Windfall.*** Whenever you use Heroic Inspiration, you regain a number of Hit Points equal to the die roll. If you are already at your Hit Point maximum when you use Heroic Inspiration, you instead gain Temporary Hit Points equal to the die roll. If you use Heroic Inspiration, and you roll higher on the die than is necessary to restore you to your Hit Point maximum, you gain any excess as Temporary Hit Points.
