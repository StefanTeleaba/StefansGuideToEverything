*Northlands Worldbook 5.5e*
# Clout
*General Feat (Prerequisites: Level 4+, Strength 13+, Inspiring Leader)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Strong Influence.*** You have Advantage on Charisma (Deception, Intimidation, and Persuasion) checks when interacting with Vikings, professional warriors, and other martial folk. When using the Inspiring Leader feat, you also add your Strength modifier to the Temporary Hit Points you grant.
