*Northlands Worldbook 5.5e*
# Bloodied Resilience
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Bloodied Reaction.*** While you're Bloodied and take any damage, you can spend one of your Hit Dice as a Reaction and reduce the damage you take by the amount rolled. You can only spend one Hit Die at a time in this way, each time you are damaged.  
***Bloodied Saves.*** If you succeed on a Strength or Constitution saving throw that would normally cause you to take half damage, you take no damage instead.
