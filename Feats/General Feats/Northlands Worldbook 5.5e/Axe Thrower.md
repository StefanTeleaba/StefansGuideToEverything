*Northlands Worldbook 5.5e*
# Axe Thrower
*General Feat (Prerequisites: Level 4+, Proficiency with Handaxes)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Returning Throw.*** When you make a ranged weapon attack with a Handaxe and miss on your attack roll, you can use a Bonus Action on the same turn to catch the weapon as it whirls back to you.
