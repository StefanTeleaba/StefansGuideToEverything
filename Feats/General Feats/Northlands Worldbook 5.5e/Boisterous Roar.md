*Northlands Worldbook 5.5e*
# Boisterous Roar
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Charisma 13+)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
***Mighty Yell.*** When you score a Critical Hit against a target, you let out a boisterous roar. You and allies within 5 feet of you have Resistance to all damage until the end of your next turn, and enemies within 5 feet of you have Vulnerability to all damage until the end of your next turn.
