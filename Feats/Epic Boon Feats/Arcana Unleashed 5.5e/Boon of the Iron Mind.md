*Arcana Unleashed 5.5e p.33*
# Boon of the Iron Mind
*Epic Boon Feat (Prerequisite: Level 19+)*  
You gain the following benefits.  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.  
***Unshakeable Focus.*** When you are maintaining Concentration on an effect, you lose Concentration only if you cast a spell or activate another effect that requires Concentration, if you have the Petrified or Unconscious Condition, or if you die.
