# Bastard Sword (20 GP)
*Martial Melee Weapon*  
**Damage:** 1d8 Slashing  
**Properties:** Versatile (1d10)  
**Mastery:** Brutal  
**Weight:** 4 lb.  
***Versatile.*** A Versatile weapon can be used with one or two hands. A damage value in parentheses appears with the property. The weapon deals that damage when used with two hands to make a melee attack.  
***Mastery: Brutal.*** A Brutal weapon is designed for maximum damage. Brutal weapons score Critical Hits on a natural roll of 19 or 20. If your Critical Range is already increased, instead increase it by 1 point (such as from 19-20 to 18-20). When you score a Critical Hit with a Brutal weapon against a creature, the weapon deals one additional die of damage.

# Bastard Sword
*Exotic Melee Weapon*  
**Cost:** 35 GP  
**Damage:** 1d10  
**Critical:** 19-20/×2
**Range Increment:** -  
**Weight:** 6 lb.  
**Type:** Slashing  
Bastard swords are also known as hand-and-a-half swords. A bastard sword is too large to use in one hand without special training; thus, it is an exotic weapon. A character can use a bastard sword two-handed as a martial weapon.
