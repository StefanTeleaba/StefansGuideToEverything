# Wall of Sand
*Level 3 Evocation (Druid, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** 90 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (a handful of sand)  
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You create a wall of swirling sand on a solid surface within range. You can make the wall up to 30 feet long, 10 feet high, and 10 feet thick, or a ringed wall up to 20 feet in diameter, 10 feet high, and 5 feet thick. The wall vanishes when the spell ends. The wall is opaque and grants Half Cover against attacks and other effects that originate from its space or that pass through it. A creature fully within the wall's space has the Blinded condition and must spend 3 feet of movement for every 1 foot it moves there.  
On your later turns, you can take a Magic action to recreate the wall on another solid surface within range.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The wall's maximum length, height, thickness, and diameter increase by 5 feet for each spell slot level above 3.

# Wall of Sand
*Level 3 Evocation (Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** 90 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (a handful of sand)  
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You create a wall of swirling sand on the ground at a point you can see within range. You can make the wall up to 30 feet long, 10 feet high, and 10 feet thick, and it vanishes when the spell ends. It blocks line of sight but not movement. A creature is blinded while in the wall's space and must spend 3 feet of movement for every 1 foot it moves there.
