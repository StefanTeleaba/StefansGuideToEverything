*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Iron Horn
*Level 1 Transmutation (Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You create a deep resonant vibration that can shake creatures off their feet. Each creature in a 30-foot Cone originating from you must succeed on a Strength saving throw or have the Prone condition.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The Cone's length increases by 5 feet for each spell slot level above 1.

*Forgotten Realms: Magic of Faerûn 3.5e p.79*  
# Balagarn's Iron Horn
*Transmutation [Sonic]*  
**Level:** Brd 1, Sor/Wiz 2  
**Components:** V, S  
**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** Close (25 ft.+ 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Area:** Cone  
**Duration:** Instantaneous  
**Saving Throw:** None (see text)  
**Spell Resistance:** Yes  
You create a deep, resonant vibration that can shake creatures off their feet as if they were being tripped. Make a single Strength check as if your Strength were 20. Creatures in the area make individual opposed Dexterity or Strength checks against your roll. Those who fail are tripped and fall prone. Those who succeed are unaffected, but cannot make a trip attack against you in response to this spell. Unlike a regular trip attack, you may trip any creature touching the ground, regardless of size. You cannot use the Improved Trip feat to immediately make a melee attack against a creature you have tripped with this spell.
