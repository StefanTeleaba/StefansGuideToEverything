*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Fracture Mind
*Level 1 Enchantment (Bard, Psion, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Components:** S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You drive a spear of psychic energy into the mind of one creature you can see within range. The target's mind fractures into psychic shards that pierce the minds of up to three other creatures of your choice, each of which must be within 30 feet of the first target. Each target makes a Wisdom saving throw, taking 3d6 Psychic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** One additional shard leaps from the first target to another target for each spell slot level above 1.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
