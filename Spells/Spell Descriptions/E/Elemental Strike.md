# Elemental Strike
*Level 3 Evocation (Ranger)*

**Casting Time:** Bonus action, which you take immediately after hitting or missing a target with a weapon  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

As your attack hits or misses the target, the weapon or ammunition you're using bursts with the power of the elements. Choose Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poison, or Thunder. The target takes 2d8 damage of the chosen type. Each creature of your choice within 15 feet of the target then makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d8 damage of the chosen type on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.  
The weapon or ammunition then returns to its normal form.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage for both effects of the spell increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 3.
