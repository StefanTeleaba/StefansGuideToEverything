*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Anger of the Noonday Sun
*Level 6 Evocation (Druid)*

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Brilliant sunlight flashes from you in a 60-foot Emanation. Each creature you choose in the Emanation makes a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes 10d6 Radiant damage and has the Blinded condition for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, the Blinded target makes a Constitution saving throw, ending the spell on itself on a success. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage only. This spell dispels Darkness in its area that was created by any spell.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 2d6 for each spell slot level above 6.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The emanation increases by 10 feet for each spell slot level above 6.

# Anger of the Noonday Sun
*Evocation [Light]*  
**Level:** Druid 6  
**Components:** V, S  
**Casting Time:** 1 standard action  
**Range:** 20 ft.  
**Area:** All sighted creatures within a 20-ft.-radius burst centered on you  
**Duration:** Instantaneous  
**Saving Throw:** Reflex negates; see text  
**Spell Resistance:** Yes  
*Your body explodes with radiance that bathes the area around you in sunlight.*  
Any creature within the area of the spell that can see you must make a saving throw or be temporarily blinded. The blindness lasts for 1 minute per caster level.  
An undead creature caught within the spell’s area takes 1d6 points of damage per two caster levels (maximum 10d6), or half damage if a Reflex save is successful. In addition, the beam results in the destruction of any undead creature specifically harmed by bright light (such as a vampire) if it fails its save.  
The ultraviolet light generated by the spell deals damage to fungi, mold, oozes, and slimes as if they were undead creatures.
