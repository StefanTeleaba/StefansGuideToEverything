*Arcana Unleashed 5.5e p.42*
# Mordenkainen's Lucubration
*Level 5 Divination (Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You recover up to two expended spell slots of level 2 or lower.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The maximum level of spell slots you can recover increases to level 3 (if
cast with a level 6-7 spell slot) or level 4 (if cast with a level 8+ spell slot).

# Mordenkainen's Lucubration
(Alteration)  
(Mentalism)  
Level: 6  
Range: 0  
Components: V, S  
Casting Time: 1  
Duration: Instantaneous  
Area of Effect: The caster  
Saving Throw: None  
By use of this spell, the wizard is able to instantly recall any 1st- through 5th-level spell he has used during the past 24 hours. The spell must have been memorized and actually used during that time period. Mordenkainen's lucubration allows the recovery of only one spell. If the recalled spell requires material components, these must be provided by the caster; the recovered spell is not usable until the material components are available.  
**Notes:** Common or uncommon spell (PHB).
