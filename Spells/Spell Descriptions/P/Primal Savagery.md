# Primal Savagery
*Transmutation Cantrip (Druid)*

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You channel primal magic to cause your teeth or fingernails to sharpen, ready to deliver a devastating attack. Make an attack with an Unarmed Strike. On a hit, the attack deals Slashing or Necrotic damage (your choice) equal to 1d8 plus the modifier you used to make the attack, instead of the normal damage for the attack. After you make the attack, your teeth or fingernails return to normal.  
If you have some kind of natural weapon that you can attack with, like a bite or claw attack, you can use that attack instead of an Unarmed Strike by temporarily augmenting those features.

***Cantrip Upgrade.*** Whether you deal Slashing or Necrotic damage, the attack deals extra damage of the same type when you reach levels 5 (1d8), 11 (2d8) and 17 (3d8).

# Primal Savagery
*Transmutation Cantrip (Druid)*

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You channel primal magic to cause your teeth or fingernails to sharpen, ready to deliver a corrosive attack. Make a melee spell attack against one creature within 5 feet of you. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 acid damage. After you make the attack, your teeth or fingernails return to normal.

The spell's damage increases by 1d10 when you reach 5th level (2d10), 11th level (3d10), and 17th level (4d10).
