*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Psychic Thorns
*Level 1 Enchantment (Bard, Psion, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Bonus Action, which you take immediately after hitting a creature with a weapon or an Unarmed Strike  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

As you hit the creature, this spell creates a burst of psychic shards that pierce its mind. The target takes an extra 2d6 Psychic damage from the attack.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1. If cast with a level 3+ spell slot, the target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the Stunned condition until the end of your next turn.
