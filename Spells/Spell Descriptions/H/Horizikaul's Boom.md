# Horizikaul's Boom
*Level 1 Evocation (Sorcerer, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** 1 minute

You unleash a blast of high pitched sound. One creature that you can see within range makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 3d8 Thunder damage and has the Deafened condition for the duration. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 1.

# Horizikaul's Boom
*Evocation [Sonic]*  
**Level:** Sor/Wiz 1  
**Components:** V, S  
**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** Close (25 ft.+ 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Target:** One creature  
**Duration:** Instantaneous  
**Saving Throw:** Will partial  
**Spell Resistance:** Yes  
You blast the target with loud and high-pitched sounds. Horizikaul's boom strikes unerringly, even if the target is in melee or has anything less than total cover or concealment. The target takes 1d4 points of sonic damage per two caster levels (minimum 1d4, maximum 5d4) and must make a Will save or be deafened for 1d4 rounds. This spell has no effect if cast into the area of a silence spell.
