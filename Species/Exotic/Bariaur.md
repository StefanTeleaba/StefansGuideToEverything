# Bariaur
Bariaurs are either born in Ysgard or have celestial ancestors who originated there. From the waist up, a bariaur resembles a humanoid such as a human, elf, or dwarf. From the waist down, they have the body of a sheep.  
Full of wanderlust, bariaurs seek adventure, treasure, and a proper revelry. Like many native inhabitants of Ysgard, bariaurs never shy away from a battle against evil and injustice. Some even carve small notches in their horns to count the number of glorious battles they have survived.  
## Bariaur Traits
**Creature Type:** Celestial  
**Size:** Medium  
**Speed:** 35 feet  
As a Bariaur, you have these special traits.  
***Darkvision.*** You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.  
***Ram.*** When you move 10 feet or more straight toward a creature and hit it with an Unarmed Strike as part of the Attack action on your turn, you can use both the Damage and Shove options. You can only use this benefit once per turn.  
***Stable Stance.*** You have Advantage on saving throws to avoid the Prone condition.  
***Wanderer's Instincts.*** You have proficiency in the Animal Handling, Arcana, Nature, or Survival skill.
