*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Minotaur
**Creature Type:** Humanoid  
**Size:** Medium (about 6-7 feet tall)  
**Speed:** 30 feet  
As a Minotaur, you have these special traits.  
***Horns.*** When you use your Unarmed Strike and deal damage, you can choose to gore with your horns. You deal Piercing damage equal to 1d4 plus your Strength modifier instead of the normal damage of an Unarmed Strike.  
***Goring Rush.*** Immediately after you take the Dash action on your turn and move 20+ feet straight toward the target, you can make one attack with your Horns as a Bonus Action.  
***Hammering Horns.*** Immediately after you hit a creature with a melee attack as part of the Attack action on your turn, you can use a Bonus Action to attempt to push that target with your horns if the target is no more than one size larger than you. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC 8 plus your Strength modifier and Proficiency Bonus), or be pushed up to 10 feet away from you.  
***Labyrinthine Recall.*** You always know which direction is north, and you have advantage on any Wisdom (Survival) check you make to navigate or track.
