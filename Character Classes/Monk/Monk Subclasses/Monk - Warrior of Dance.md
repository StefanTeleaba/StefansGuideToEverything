# Warrior of Dance
*Dance through the danger with a trusted partner.*  
Masters of posture, precision and grace, they focus on the perfection of movement and rhythm, mixing martial arts with the art of dance.

## Level 3: Rhythm Muse
You gain proficiency in the Performance skill if you don't already have it. If you are already Proficient, you can gain Proficiency in a different skill from the Monk’s skill options at level 1.  
You gain a bonus to your Charisma (Performance) checks equal to your Dexterity Modifier.

## Level 3: Tempo Shift
At the start of your turn, you can spend 1 focus point to take a dancing stance for 1 minute. The effects of a dancing stance are suppressed if your movement is reduced to 0, and end early if you have the Incapacitated condition.  
For the duration, choose one of the effects from the list below. You can spend 5 feet of movement at the start of your turn to change the active effect for a different one.  
***Heated Step.*** Whenever a creature you can see moves to a space within 5 feet of you, you can take a Reaction to make a Melee Weapon Attack or Unarmed Strike against it.  
***Dazzling Step.*** You gain a +1 Bonus to your Armor Class, and gain Advantage on Saving Throws to avoid the Prone condition or being moved against your will.  
***Twirling Step.*** Once per turn, when you hit with an attack, you can move 5 feet in any direction without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

## Level 6: Partner Up
When you roll Initiative, you can choose one ally you can see within 20 feet of you and designate it as your Dance Partner. For the next minute, your Dance Partner gains the following benefits:  
***Shared Step.*** Your Dance Partner gains the benefits of your active Tempo Shift while it is within 20 feet of you.  
***Guided Step.*** Your Dance Partner gains a bonus to Charisma (Performance) and Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks equal to your Wisdom Modifier.  
***Keep Up.*** Whenever you end your turn, your Dance Partner can take a Reaction to move up to their speed towards you without provoking Opportunity Attacks.  
You can switch your chosen Dance Partner by taking a Bonus Action.

## Level 11: Flourishing Steps
You gain the following benefits:  
***Graceful.*** When you use Step of the Wind, you gain a bonus to your Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks equal to your Wisdom Modifier.  
***Shifting Defense.*** When you use Patient Defense, you can take a Reaction to make an Opportunity Attack against a creature whenever it misses you with an attack roll.  
***Whirlwind Twirl.*** Whenever you use Flurry of Blows, you can replace that feature’s attacks with a forceful whirlwind. Each creature of your choice within 10 feet of you must make a Dexterity Saving Throw. On a failure, it takes 4 rolls of your Martial Arts die in Slashing or Force damage (your choice), and it is pushed 10 feet directly away from you.  
This damage increases by an additional roll of your martial arts die when you reach Monk Level 17.

## Level 17: Motion In Flow
You gain the following benefits:  
***Dancer’s Boon.*** Whenever you or your Dance Partner takes a Reaction, you can spend 2 Focus Points to restore the use of that Reaction. A creature can only take one Reaction per turn.  
Once a creature benefits from this feature, it can’t benefit from it again until it finishes a Short or Long Rest.  
***Mesmerizing Presence.*** While your Tempo Shift is active and your Dance Partner is within 20 feet of you, Attack Rolls against you can’t benefit from Advantage.
