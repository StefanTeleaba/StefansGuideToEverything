# Nomad
*Shape Your World Through Travel and Opportunity*  
Many nomads share one core ideal; experience the world and harness its diversity for the benefit of yourself and those dear to you. Warriors from these tribes use their mastery and status to utilize favors and edges both in and out of battle.

## Level 3: Favor
Wherever you are, you gather favors of those around you, to call upon in times of need. Whenever you finish a Long Rest, roll a number of d6s equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of one) and record the numbers rolled. These are your Favors. Each Favor can only be used once. When you finish a Long Rest, you lose any unused Favors.  
***Calling in Favors.*** Immediately before you or an ally you can see within 5 feet of yourself, make a D20 Test you can choose and expend one of your Favors and add it to the test (no action required). You can only add a Favor in this way once per turn, and you can’t use a Favor on a D20 Test if you use the Help action to assist with that test.  
***Regaining and Replacing Favors.*** You can roll one new Favor whenever you use the Help action and the ally you assist succeeds on its roll. You can have a maximum number of Favor equal to your Charisma (minimum of one). In addition, you can expend a use of your Second Wind to reroll any of your unused Favors of your choice.

## Level 3: Wandering Soul
You gain the following benefits.  
***Cultured.*** Your travels have taught you a wide variety of life lessons. You know one language of your choice and you have proficiency in one skill and one type of artisan’s tools of your choice.  
***Trader’s Charm.*** You have Advantage on Wisdom (Insight) and Charisma (Persuasion) checks when speaking to merchants, traders and other types of bartering folk about their wares and business.  
***Nomadic Fighting.*** Just like life itself, in combat you never stay still. When you hit a target with a weapon, that target can’t make Opportunity Attacks against you until the start of your next turn. You can expend one Favor as part of this attack to distract the target. If you do, the target can’t make Opportunity Attacks against any creature until the start of your next turn instead.

## Level 7: Expertise
You gain Expertise in two of your skill proficiencies of your choice.

## Level 7: Fellowship In Battle
When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can replace one of those attacks with the Help action.

## Level 10: Cosmopolitan
Your extensive travels and experiences grant you the following benefits.  
***Diligence.*** When you use one or more Hit Dice or use your Second Wind to regain Hit Points, you can expend a Favor to add it to the healing roll. If you do, you can also reduce your Exhaustion level by 1, if you have any.  
***Suave.*** Your disarming personality works wonders for you. Your Favors are now d8s, instead of d6s.

## Level 10: Stronger Together
When an ally hits a creature that you can see within your reach with an attack roll, you can take a Reaction and expend a Favor to make an Opportunity Attack against that creature, adding the expended Favor to the damage roll.

## Level 15: Resourcefulness
When you use Diligence, you can also clear one of the following conditions on yourself: Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, or Grappled.  
If the value of the expended Favor is a 6 or higher, you can clear one from the following conditions instead: Paralyzed, Poisoned, Restrained or Stunned.

## Level 18: Globetrotter
You gain the following benefits.  
***Around The World.*** Your experiences shape you and you shape your experiences. Your Proficiency Bonus permanently increases by 1.  
***Lucrative.*** You know how to make the most of any situation. Your Favors are now d10s.
