# Sword Surfer
*Surf the dunes as if they were still the old waves of the long forgotten ocean.*  
The Sword Surfer is all about surfing the sands of the desert as a nomadic warrior from a long forgotten tradition, using their vary blade to glide across the dusty landscape. Call upon the Old Sea and channel the waves that once raged as you rush down your enemies while surfing the dunes.

## Level 3: Sword of the Old Sea
You carve runes into a blade, bonding it to your very soul. Over the course of a long rest, you either find or create a special Bonded Blade out of any martial melee weapon. With your Bonded Blade, you can perform various Tricks during combat. Your Bonded Blade has the following features.  
***Blade Surf.*** You can use your Bonded Blade to surf across surfaces. You can mount or dismount the weapon by entering or leaving its space, costing you no extra movement. When mounted on your bonded weapon, The blade hovers 1 foot off the ground, and your speed is increased by 10 feet. You are still able to make normal weapon attacks while mounted on your Bonded Blade.  
***Blade Tricks.*** While mounted on your Bonded Blade, you can perform various Tricks. Tricks can only be used once per turn, replacing one Attack Action per turn. Any Trick that calls for a saving throw will use a DC equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your weapon’s attribute bonus. In combat, you gain the following options.  
***Power Slide.*** If you have moved at least 15 feet on your turn while mounted on your Bonded Blade, you can take the Attack Action to perform a Power Slide. Each creature within a 5 foot radius of your stopping point must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Trick Save DC. On failed save, a target takes Bludgeoning damage equal to your Strength modifier (minimum 1) and has the Prone condition. On a successful save, a target only takes the Bludgeoning damage.
**Kick Flip:** When you take the Attack action, you can perform a kick flip on your Bonded Blade. One creature within 5 feet of you must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Trick Save DC. On a failed save, it takes damage from the Bonded Blade as if you had hit it with an attack, it is pushed 10 feet directly away from you, and it has the Prone condition. On a successful save, the target is only knocked back 10 feet.

## Level 3: Art of the Surf
You can use your blade surfing tricks to awe the crowds, giving your actions some sway in situations that may require words.  
***Skill Proficiency.*** You gain proficiency in Acrobatics.  
***Sword Flair.*** When making an Charisma ability check that requires Performance, Persuasion, or Intimidation, you can make an Acrobatics Check instead.

## Level 7: Gift of the Old Sea
While riding your Bonded Blade, the Old Sea that guides you has gifted you with unique abilities. While mounted on your Bonded Blade, you gain the following features.  
***Wall Rider.*** When mounted on your bonded weapon, you gain a climb speed equal to half your speed.  
***Steady Balance.*** You have advantages on all rolls to avoid the Prone condition Nose Grab. Opportunity Attacks made against you while using the Dash action are done so at Disadvantage.

## Level 10: Surging Sands
You have improved greatly in your blade surfing, and begun to evolve your tricks for better results. Your Powerslide and Kickflip have improved into new attacks.  
***Cutback.*** When you perform a Power Slide, at the end of your Movement you can take a bonus action to attack a single creature within 5 feet of your stopping point as part of the slide. Any remaining movement spent after this attack will not provoke Attacks of Opportunity.  
***Heel Flip.*** When you perform a Kick Flip, On a failed save, the target takes damage from the Bonded Blade as if you had hit them with an attack, are pushed 15 feet away, and have the Prone condition. On a successful save, they are pushed back 15 feet and have disadvantage on their next attack roll.

## Level 15: Master of the Rolling Sands
You have mastered the art of blade surfing as a form of combat. You can now chain tricks together. When you take the Attack action, you can now replace any number of your attacks with Tricks. You can't use the same Trick twice in a row.

## Level 18: Memories of the Forgotten Sea
The Sword of the Old Sea has granted you its final boon. Harness the power of the Old Sea as your Tricks improve once more. You can use this feature once per combat and regain use once you have completed a long rest.  
***Tsunami.*** When performing Power Slide, a wave of sea water follows you. Each creature within a 10 foot radius of your stopping point must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes Bludgeoning damage equal to your Strength modifier (Minimum of 1) + Cold damage equal to half your Fighter level and is given the Stunned Condition until the start of your next turn. On a successful roll, the target only takes the Cold damage and their speed is reduced by half. Any creature that is below half of their health points caught in the radius have disadvantage on their Dexterity Saving throw.  
***Whirlpool.*** When performing a Kick flip, any creature within a 15 foot radius of you is forced to make a Dexterity saving throw against your Trick Save DC. On a failed roll, all creatures caught in the radius take Slashing damage equal to half your Fighter level, are pushed 15 feet away, and are given the Prone Condition. On a successful save, the target creatures only take the slashing damage. Any creature that is below half of their health points caught in the radius have disadvantage on their Dexterity Saving throw.
