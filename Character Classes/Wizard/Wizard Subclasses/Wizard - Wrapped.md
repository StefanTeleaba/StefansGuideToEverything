# Wrapped
*By the eternal sands and the scorching sun, I curse you to wander under the sting of a thousand scorpions!*  
Some have stumbled across this power by accident, when looting an ancient tomb, while others have inherited a tradition of old. Regardless, a wrapped is a master of protective magic and retributive curses, inspired by the magical defenses put in place to ward off intruders and thieves in royal tombs, and to guide the dead safely along their voyage through the Underworld.

## Level 3: Book of the Dead
When others took the gold and other shiny trinkets, you found another kind of treasure, wrapping the mummy tight.  
You gain an additional Spellbook, made of long strips of linen wrapped around your body.  
When you gain this feature, all Abjuration, Divination, and Necromancy spells currently in your spellbook are automatically copied into these wrappings at no cost.  
Whenever you copy an Abjuration, Divination, or Necromancy spell into this spellbook, you need spend only 1 hour and 10 GP for each level of the copied spell.

## Level 3: Cursed Wrappings
The arcane inscription on your wrappings foretell the horrible fate of those that try to harm you.  
***Hexed Fate.*** You always have the *Hex* spell prepared, and it counts as a Wizard spell for you. You can cast it without expending a spell slot a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest. When you cast *Hex* using this feature, the spell doesn't require Concentration.  
***Magic Linen.*** When you make a Melee Spell Attack, your reach for that attack is 5 feet greater than normal.

## Level 6: Inscribe Linen
As you delve deep in these old, mystic scripts, you learn how to hide more spells into your wrappings.  
Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can choose Abjuration, Divination, or Necromancy spells in your spellbook whose combined spell levels equal 6 or less. The chosen spells count as prepared for you until the end of your next Long Rest, and they don't count against the maximum number of spells you can prepare.

## Level 10: Preserved Life
Despite not being as effective as a lich ritual, you have found ways to extend your life, at least in the case of danger. You gain the following benefits.  
***Warded Essence.*** You gain Resistance to Necrotic damage.  
***Jarred Vitality.*** Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can consume 100 GP of precious materials per jar to create up to three jars containing portions of your essence. You can have a maximum of three jars active at a time. The jars retain their potency until consumed:  
**Death Save.** When you fail a Death Saving Throw, you can expend one jar to replace the failure with a success.  
**Instant Death.** When an effect would kill you instantly without dealing damage or when you suffer massive damage that would kill you outright, you can expend three jars to instead drop to 1 Hit Point.

## Level 14: Curse of the Eons
To preserve a longer life, you have learned ways to siphon it from miserable souls, trying to conquer you and your treasures.  
As a Magic action, you can target one creature you can see within 60 feet of yourself. The target must succeed on an Intelligence saving throw against your spell save DC or take the Incapacitated and Prone conditions for 1 minute.  
While the target is affected by this feature, it takes Necrotic damage equal to 1d8 + your Intelligence modifier at the start of each of your turns, and you regain Hit Points equal to the damage taken.  
The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. Alternatively, another creature within reach of the target can take an Action to make a Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check against your spell save DC, freeing the target on a success.  
If the effect ends early because of a successful saving throw or ability check, the creature that ended the effect takes 10d8 + your Intelligence modifier Necrotic damage, and you gain Temporary Hit Points equal to the damage taken.  
Damage dealt by this feature ignores Resistance and Immunity to Necrotic damage. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest.
