# Sphinx Patron
*Puzzle your Enemies and Reveal Untold Truth*  
Warlocks with the Sphinx Patron are seekers of knowledge, and use their knowledge to obfuscate truth and lies. Like the sphinx, a Warlock of the Sphinx will challenge the mental fortitude of those around them.

## Level 3: Curse of Riddles
Your patron grants you exquisite insight into complexity that others may simply not comprehend. As a Bonus Action, you can manifest a Curse of Riddles in a creature’s mind. The curse lasts for 1 minute, or until you dismiss it (no action required), gain the Incapacitated condition, or use this feature again.  
You can use Curse of Riddles a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of 1) and regain all expended uses on a Long Rest.  
When you manifest a Curse of Riddles, the affected creature must make an Intelligence saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failure, choose one of the following riddles to affect the target:  
***Riddle of Truths.*** The creature cannot speak a deliberate lie for the duration. An affected creature is aware of this and can avoid answering questions to which it would normally respond with a lie. Such a creature can be evasive yet must be truthful.  
***Riddle of Lies.*** Whenever the affected creature makes an attack or forces a creature to make a saving throw, you can use your Reaction to deal 1d10 plus your Warlock level of Psychic damage to them.

## Level 3: Sphinx’s Lore
Whenever you make an Intelligence check, you gain a bonus to the check equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of +1).

## Level 3: Sphinx Spells
The magic of your patron ensures you always have certain spells ready; when you reach a Warlock level specified in the Sphinx Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.  
**Sphinx Spells Table**
| Warlock Level | Spells                                                            |

| 5             | Dispel Magic, Hypnotic Pattern                                    |
| 7             | Confusion, Divination                                             |

## Level 6: Friends and Foes
You have Resistance to Psychic damage.  
Additionally, whenever you use Curse of Riddles on a target, you can also choose one of the following effects to add to the curse.  
***Riddle of Friends.*** The creature gains the Charmed condition for the duration. The creature can repeat the saving throw at the start of its turns, ending the Charmed condition on itself with a success. The Charmed condition also ends early if you make an attack roll against the creature or force it to make a saving throw.  
***Riddle of Foes.*** The creature feels itself become surrounded and its fears become manifest. The creature gains the Frightened condition for the duration. The creature can repeat the saving throw at the start of its turns, ending the Frightened condition on itself with a success.

## Level 10: Scrutinous Divination
When you finish a Long Rest, you can prepare one Divination spell with a spell level of 5 or lower from the Cleric, Druid, Warlock, or Wizard spell lists. You can cast this spell once without expending a spell slot, and can also cast it by expending a Pact Magic spell slot. You can only have one spell prepared in this way and it doesn’t count towards your total number of prepared spells.

## Level 14: The Great Secret
Your patron has entrusted you with the secret they guard. Such knowledge has insight and power beyond reality.  
As a Magic Action, you touch a creature and imbue them with the power behind this knowledge. For 10 minutes, or until you gain the Incapacitated condition, the affected creature gains one of the following benefits:  
***Beyond Capability.*** The affected creature chooses one Ability Score of their choice. The chosen Ability Score increases by 2, to a maximum of 22, for the duration.  
***Mastery.*** Whenever the affected creature makes a skill check using a skill to which they are proficient, they roll with Advantage.  
***Foresight.*** Attack rolls against the affected creature have Disadvantage and they cannot be Surprised.
