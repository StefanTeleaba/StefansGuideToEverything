# Pharaoh Sorcery
*Wield the Majesty of Desert Kings*  
The magic in your veins stems from a gilded bloodline tied to ancient desert kings. Where you tread, the divine authority of the sand and sun rises to enforce your command, shielding you from harm and striking down those who defy your reign.

## Level 3: Pharaoh Spells
When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Pharaoh Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

## Level 3: Crook and Flail
While your Innate Sorcery is active, a spectral Crook and Flail appear beside you. When you activate your Innate Sorcery, and as a Bonus Action while the feature is active, you can command either the Crook or Flail to assail a creature you can see within 10 feet of yourself. Alternatively, even if your Innate Sorcery isn't active, immediately after you spend at least 1 Sorcery Point as part of the Magic action on your turn, you can take a Bonus Action to temporarily conjure the effect of either the crook or flail, choosing one of the targets of that Magic action for the effect.  
***Crook.*** The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your spell save DC or be moved up to 10 feet in a direction of your choice.  
***Flail.*** You make a melee spell attack against the target. On a hit, it takes Force damage equal to 1d8 plus your Charisma modifier (minimum of 1).

## Level 6: Scarab Shell
When you are hit by an attack from a creature within 10 feet of you, you can use your Reaction to rapidly interpose a wall of spectral scarabs. You gain a bonus to your AC equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of 1) against that attack, potentially causing it to miss.  
Whether the attack hits or misses, the scarabs disperse and settle over you as armor. You gain Temporary Hit Points equal to half your Sorcerer level (rounded down).  
You can use this ability a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of 1). You regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

## Level 14: Desert Sovereignty
Your connection to the desert monarchs empowers your regalia, expanding their reach and authority in the following ways:
- The range of your Scarab Shell and both the Crook and Flail increases to 15 feet.
- The damage of the Flail increases to 2d8 + your Charisma modifier Force damage.
- When a creature fails its saving throw against the Crook, you can choose to knock it Prone in addition to moving it.

## Level 18: Dynasty of the Sun
A ruler deserves subjects as eternal as their empire. You gain the following benefits:  
***Embalming.*** When a creature within 30 feet of you is reduced to 0 Hit Points, you can take a Reaction and spend 3 Sorcery Points to transform its corpse into an undead servant. You instantly cast the Summon Undead spell without expending a spell slot or requiring material components, choosing the Putrid form. The spell doesn't require Concentration when cast this way, and it ends early if you use this feature again.  
***Eternal Preservation.*** You cease aging. You have Advantage on saving throws against being Poisoned, and you have Resistance to Poison damage. Additionally, you don’t need to eat, drink, or breathe.
