# Sandsworn Sorcery
*Raise Legions from Dust and Sand*  
Your magic transforms eroded earth and minerals into echoes of the fallen empires they once belonged to, lifetimes ago. Through sheer will, you can shape mere sand into loyal troops, and even dust can serve as the building blocks for kingdoms once more. Perhaps you are descended from a ruler of one of these ancient powers, or you are the result of a desperate attempt to resurrect one of these empires from the grave.

## Level 3: Sandsworn Spells
When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Sandsworn Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

## Level 3: Sandsworn Battalion
When you use Innate Sorcery, you can summon a Medium sized Sand Sentry within 30 feet of yourself that lasts until your Innate Sorcery ends. The number of Sand Sentries you can summon in this way increases when you reach Sorcerer levels 5 (two), 11 (three), and 17 (four).  
As a Bonus Action on your subsequent turns, you can cause one of your Sand Sentries to move to a space within 30 feet of yourself. You can also take a Magic action to move all of your Sand Sentries in this way at once.  
Immediately after a Sand Sentry moves or is summoned, you can command it to strike a creature within 10 feet of it. Make a melee spell attack as if you were in the Sand Sentry's space. On a hit, the creature takes Force damage equal to 1d6 plus your Charisma modifier.

## Level 6: Dividing Phalanx
You can cast Wall of Sand without expending a spell slot. When you cast it in this way, it doesn't require Concentration, but it lasts for 1 minute and ends early if you cast it again. If you place the wall within 15 feet of yourself, you can have it take the form of a Phalanx that charges up to 30 feet in a direction of your choice, and forces each creature of your choice in its path to succeed on a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC or be pushed along with it.  
Once you cast Wall of Sand in this way, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest. You can also restore your use of it by spending 3 Sorcery Points (no action required).

## Level 6: Torrid Sentinels
Once per turn, when a creature enters or leaves a space within 10 feet of one of your Sand Sentries, you can command it to strike that creature as if it just moved or was summoned. On a hit, the creature has its Speed reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

## Level 14: Desert Step
Whenever you enter a space within 30 feet of one of your Sand Sentries, one of your Sand Sentries enters a space within 30 feet of you, or you are hit by an attack roll, you can take a Reaction to move up to your Speed towards one of your Sand Sentries without provoking Opportunity Attacks. If you end this movement within 5 feet of one of your Sand Sentries, you also gain Temporary Hit Points equal to 1d4 plus your Sorcerer level.

## Level 18: Monument of a Bygone Era
As a Magic action, you can erect a Huge siege tower of solid sand in an unoccupied space on the ground within 60 feet of yourself that lasts for 10 minutes. The tower can be up to 30 feet tall and its AC is 19. It has a number of Hit Points equal to five times your Sorcerer level.  
At the end of each of your turns, you can command the tower to strike a target of your choice within 120 feet of it. Make a ranged spell attack as if you were at its peak. On a hit, the target takes 4d10 Force damage.  
Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest. You can also regain a use of this feature by expending a level 5+ spell slot (no action required).
