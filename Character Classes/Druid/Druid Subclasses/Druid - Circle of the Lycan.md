# Circle of the Lycan
*Tear with Fang & Sunder with Spellcraft*  
Druids of the Circle of the Lycan perform foul, accursed rites to hijack their ability to Wild Shape, allowing them to transform into twised hybrids of their normal forms and that of nature’s creations. Often mistaken for lycanthropes, these outcasts rend their foes with sharpened fang and claw, in terrifying tandem with spellcraft.

## Level 3: Circle of the Lycan Spells

## Level 3: Hybrid Form
When you use your Wild Shape feature, after selecting which Beast you want to transform into, you can choose to turn into a hybrid between your normal form and that of the beast you chose. Using your Hybrid Form affects Wild Shape in the following ways.  
***Duration.*** The duration of the transformation changes to 10 minutes.  
***Temporary Hit Points.*** You gain a number of Temporary Hit Points equal to twice your Druid level.  
***Game Statistics.*** Your body changes in ways such that you gain the Beast’s Speeds, its senses, its Traits, and its Actions, Bonus Actions and Reactions, but no other elements of its stat block.  
***Bestial Strikes.*** When you make an attack from one of the Beast’s Actions, Bonus Actions or Reactions, you can use your spell attack bonus instead of the normal attack bonus, and you can add your Wisdom modifier to the damage roll, instead of the normal damage bonus. In addition, you can use your spell save DC in place of any DCs included in the stat block.  
***Spellcasting.*** Shape-shifting in this way does not prevent you from casting your Druid spells.

## Level 6: Hybrid Attacks
While you are in your Hybrid Form, if you take the Attack action using one of your Bestial Strikes and don’t use Multiattack, you can do one of the following as part of the same action.
- You can take one additional action from the Beast’s stat block. This action cannot benefit from Multiattack.
- You can cast one of your Druid cantrips with an action casting time.

## Level 6: Death Touch
While you are in your Hybrid Form, whenever you deal damage with your Bestial Strikes, you can change the damage type to Necrotic.

## Level 10: Accursed Growth
Your lycanthropic transformation now causes an immense growth of new flesh, granting you the following benefits while in your Hybrid Form.  
***Growth.*** When you transform you can choose to become Large. While Large, you can use your Wisdom modifier in place of your Strength or Constitution whenever you make a D20 Test using one of those abilities.  
***Regeneration.*** When you start your turn in your Hybrid Form, you gain a number of Temporary Hit Points equal to half your Druid level (round up).

## Level 14: Bestial Spellcraft
While you are in your Hybrid Form, whenever you take the Magic action to cast a spell, you can take a Bonus Action to make a Bestial Strike that would normally require an action.  
Starting at Druid level 18, you can make two Bestial Strikes with this Bonus Action, instead of one.
