# Circle of Erosion
*Wear Down Foes with Nature's Patience*  
Druids of the Circle of Erosion embody nature at its slowest, but most far reaching. Even a constant breeze or small river can reshape entire landscapes over time, and it is these forces that these Druids channel, letting them slowly but surely reduce even the tallest buildings and stoutest foes to dust.

## Level 3: Nature’s Sculptor
As a Bonus Action, you can expend one use of your Wild Shape to conjure a torrent of water, wind, or sand which orbits around your body for 10 minutes. It ends early if you dismiss it (no action required), use this feature again, or have the Incapacitated condition.  
When you activate this feature or as a Bonus Action on your subsequent turns, you can target one object that isn’t being worn or carried or one creature that you can see within 30 feet of yourself. The target takes Acid or Slashing damage (your choice) and suffers from Erosion. To determine this damage, roll a number of d6s equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1d6). A target creature can make a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC to take half damage only on a success.  
A target with Erosion suffers the appropriate penalty below.  
***Armored.*** If the target is a creature wearing armor, the AC of that armor is reduced by 1 until it is repaired over the course of a Long Rest, or is targeted by the *Mending* spell, ending the Erosion.  
***Unarmored.*** If the target is a creature that isn’t wearing armor, its flesh becomes raw until it regains any Hit Points, ending the Erosion. Once per turn, when it takes damage of a type other than Psychic, the damage increases by 1d6.  
***Object.*** If the target is an object, it takes twice as much damage. If you reduce it to 0 Hit Points with this feature you can carve it into a statuette in any shape you describe that is smaller in all dimensions than the original object.

## Level 6: Patient Inevitability
Erosion is a slow process, but it is persistent - it will prevail eventually, and so will you. Whenever a creature succeeds on a saving throw against one of your Druid spells or features, you can give it a -1 penalty to its next save against one of those effects. This penalty accumulates by a further -1 each time the target succeeds on its save until it eventually fails, you give a different creature this penalty, or it finishes a Long Rest.

## Level 10: Enhanced Erosion
You can now progress the erosion you inflict on your foes. When a creature that is already Eroded fails its saving throw against your Nature’s Sculptor feature, the penalty it experiences now worsens, gaining an additional level of Erosion. A creature’s Erosion level can’t exceed 2 or your Wisdom modifier, whichever is higher.  
***Armored.*** The armor’s AC is reduced by 1 for each Erosion level. If the armor isn’t magical and its AC is reduced to the point it is providing no AC bonus to its wearer, it is destroyed and the target’s Erosion ends.  
***Unarmored.*** The extra damage a creature takes increases by 1d6 for each Erosion level beyond the first.

## Level 14: Expedited Attrition
Once per turn when a creature fails its saving throw against one of your Circle of Erosion spells and takes damage, you can cause it to take an additional 1d6 Acid or Slashing damage (your choice) and gain one level of Erosion.
