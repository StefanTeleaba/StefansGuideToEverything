# Gloom Stalker

## Level 3: Dark Words
You use shadowy language and horrifying threats to make yourself imposing or hide your intentions. Whenever you make a Deception or Intimidation check, you gain a bonus to the check equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of +1).

## Level 3: Night Terror
You have adapted to the darkness and use the shadows to terrify your foes.  
***Darkvision.*** You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet. If you already have Darkvision when you gain this feature, its range increases by 60 feet.  
***Dreadful Strikes.*** Once per turn, when you hit a creature with an attack roll, you can force it to make a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, the target has the Frightened condition until the end of your next turn.

## Level 3: Gloom Stalker Magic
When you reach a Ranger level specified in the Gloom Stalker Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.  
**Gloom Stalker Spells**
| Ranger Level | Spells                                  |
|:------------:|-----------------------------------------|
| 3            | Bane, Disguise Self                     |
| 5            | Darkness, Pass without Trace            |
| 9            | Nondetection, Fear                      |
| 13           | Greater Invisibility, Phantasmal Killer |
| 17           | Mislead, Seeming                        |

## Level 7: Iron Mind
Your expertise in the art of terrifying has hardened your mind. You gain proficiency in Wisdom saving throws. If you already have this proficiency, you instead gain proficiency in Intelligence or Charisma saving throws (your choice).

## Level 11: Menacing Shadow
Enemies see you in every shadow. When you cause a creature to gain the Frightened condition, you can scare it stiff. While Frightened, the creature has a Speed of 0 and Disadvantage on all ability checks and attack rolls, even when it cannot see you.  
You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

## Level 15: Master of Fear
Your mastery over your enemies' fear gives you the following benefits:  
***Mass Fear.*** When you use Dreadful Strikes, you can also force all creatures of your choice within 10 feet of the target to make the saving throw.  
***Stalker's Flurry.*** When you hit a creature with an attack, you deal an additional 1d8 Psychic damage to the target.

# Gloom Stalker
*Draw on Shadow Magic to Fight Your Foes*  
Gloom Stalkers are at home in the darkest places, wielding magic drawn from the Shadowfell to com- bat enemies that lurk in darkness.

## Level 3: Dread Ambusher
You have mastered the art of creating fearsome ambushes, granting you the following benefits.  
***Ambusher's Leap.*** At the start of your first turn of each combat, your Speed increases by 10 feet until the end of that turn.  
***Dreadful Strike.*** When you attack a creature and hit it with a weapon, you can deal an extra 2d6 Psychic damage. You can use this benefit only once per turn, you can use it a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.  
***Initiative Bonus.*** When you roll Initiative, you can add your Wisdom modifier to the roll.

## Level 3: Gloom Stalker Spells
When you reach a Ranger level specified in the Gloom Stalker Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.  
**Gloom Stalker Spells**
| Ranger Level | Spells               |
|:------------:|----------------------|
| 3            | Disguise Self        |
| 5            | Rope Trick           |
| 9            | Fear                 |
| 13           | Greater Invisibility |
| 17           | Seeming              |

## Level 3: Umbral Sight
You gain Darkvision with a range of 60 feet. If you already have Darkvision when you gain this feature, its range increases by 60 feet.  
You may take the Hide action as a Bonus Action, and may take this action if Heavily Obscured by Darkness even if you can be seen as a result of Darkvision or Truesight.  
When you have the Invisible Condition as a result of the Hide action, you cannot be seen by a creature with Truesight.

## Level 7: Iron Mind
You have honed your ability to resist mind-altering powers. You gain proficiency in Wisdom saving throws. If you already have this proficiency, you instead gain proficiency in Intelligence or Charisma saving throws (your choice).  
Whenever you make a Saving Throw for Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma and are Invisible as a result of the Hide Action, you gain a bonus to the roll equal to your Wisdom modifier.

## Level 11: Stalker's Fury
Whenever you are Invisible as a result of the Hide Action, and you make a Dreadful Strike, it does not count against your number of uses per Long Rest. In addition, when you use the Dreadful Strike effect of your Dread Ambusher feature, you can cause one of the following additional effects.  
***Sudden Strike.*** You can make another attack with the same weapon against a different creature that is within 5 feet of the original target and that is within the weapon's range.  
***Mass Fear.*** The target and each creature within 30 feet of it must make a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, a creature has the Frightened condition until the start of your next
turn.  
If you are Invisible as a result of the Hide Action when you hit a creature with a Dreadful Strike, you may apply both Sudden Strike and Mass Fear to the same attack.

## Level 15: Shadowy Dodge
When you are Invisible as a result of the Hide action and are hit by an attack, you may use your Reaction to cause that attack to miss instead.
