# Mirage Weaver
*Lead Your Foes to False Hope*  
You have become a Mirage Weaver by spending time in the desert. You learn to fabricate illusions that shimmer like the desert mirages which entice the desperate and foolhardy to their doom.

## Level 3: Mirage Weaver Spells
When you reach a Ranger level specified in the Mirage Weaver Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.  
**Mirage Weaver Spells**
| Ranger Level | Spells            |
|:------------:|-------------------|
| 3            | Silent Image      |
| 5            | Mirror Image      |
| 9            | Major Image       |
| 13           | Phantasmal Killer |
| 17           | Mislead           |

## Level 3: Fata Morgana
You can expend one use of your Favored Foe to cast the Silent Image spell. When you cast it this way, you can modify it so it entices a creature within 60 feet of the illusion. The target must succeed on an Intelligence saving throw against your spell save DC or be subjected to one of the following effects for 1 minute. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. The chosen effect ends early if the illusion disappears or if they discern the illusion is false.  
***Alluring Oasis.*** The creature has the Charmed condition. While Charmed this way, at the start of each of its turns, the target begins moving towards the illusion by the shortest and most direct route.  
***Distant Threat.*** The creature has the Frightened condition.  
***Hazy Hazard.*** Once per turn, when you hit the creature with an attack or deal damage with a spell or cantrip, it takes an 1d6 additional Psychic damage. When you reach Ranger level 20, the damage increases to a d10.  
When you reach Ranger level 9, you can expend 2 uses of your Favored Foe to cast Mirror Image and modify it with this feature. When you reach Ranger level 13, taking damage can’t break your Concentration on the spell modified by this feature.

## Level 3: Misleading Techniques
You gain the benefits below.  
***Elusive Images.*** Whenever you take a Magic Action to move an illusion you created with a level 1+ spell, you can do so as a Bonus Action.  
***Minor Illusion.*** You know the *Minor Illusion* cantrip.  
***Skill Proficiency.*** Choose one of the following skills: Deception, Investigation, or Persuasion. You have proficiency in that skill.

## Level 7: Reality Shift
Whenever a creature succeeds on an ability check to discern an illusion you created with a level 1+ spell, you can force them to take Psychic damage equal to 1d6 times the spell level (minimum of 1d6) plus half your Ranger level (rounded down).

## Level 11: Hallucinated Protection
While you are within 5 feet of an illusion you created with a level 1+ spell, you gain the following benefits. If you take damage or fail a saving throw, these effects cease to function until the start of your next turn.  
***Elusive Being.*** Creatures have Disadvantage on ability checks made to locate you.  
***Shimmer Shelter.*** Creatures have Disadvantage on attacks made against you.

## Level 15: Projected Self
You always have the Project Self spell prepared. You can cast the spell once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in this way when you finish a Long Rest. Whenever you cast the spell, you can modify it so it gains the following effects.  
***Mirror Strikes.*** Whenever you take the Attack action to make an attack or cast a spell, you can do so as if you were in the illusion’s space.  
***Reinforced Projection.*** The illusion shares your AC and saving throws. It also gains a number of Hit Points equal to four times your Ranger level.
