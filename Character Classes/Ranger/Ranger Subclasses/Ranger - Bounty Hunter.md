# Bounty Hunter
*Hunt Down Villains, Collect their Bounties*  
Bounty Hunters are experts at tracking and capturing dangerous fugitives, a task they perform in exchange for lucrative bounties. Once set on a quarry, the hunter’s every waking minute is devoted to their target, relentlessly hunting them down.  
Depending on the nature of the bounty, these Rangers may employ tactics to capture their target alive to return them to the client, or to execute them on the spot. For both tasks, they utilise special tools, magic or some combination thereof.  
**Flavor Suggestion**  
While this subclass makes use of spells like *Hunter’s Mark* and magical abilities like Restricting Strike to represent the Bounty Hunter’s particular brand of skills and arsenal, you might consider flavouring these abilities as a practiced skill in tracking your quarries, and traps and equipment you use to capture them.

## Level 3: Bounty Hunter Spells
Your training as a Bounty Hunter ensures you always have certain spells ready; when you reach a Ranger level specified in the Bounty Hunter Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

## Level 3: Bounty Hunter’s Tactics
You are an expert at tracking down your quarries from far distances, and capturing them alive or dead. Your Favored Enemy feature improves in the following ways.  
***Designate Quarry.*** You can target a creature with your *Hunter’s Mark* spell even if you can’t see it, and from any range, so long as it is on the same plane of existence as you. To do so, you must know what the creature looks like and either its name or an alias it goes by.  
***Kill or Capture.*** When you move your *Hunter’s Mark* onto a creature, you decide whether you intend to **Kill** or **Capture** the target. Your choice grants you certain benefits while that creature remains marked by the spell.  
**Capture**  
***Restricting Strike.*** Once per turn when you hit the target of your *Hunter’s Mark* with an attack, you can force it to make a Strength saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, it has the Grappled condition as ropes, vines or chains enwrap it. The grapple’s escape DC is equal to your spell save DC. The condition ends early if the spell ends on the target.  
***Preserve Quarry.*** If you see the creature marked by your *Hunter’s Mark* drop to 0 Hit Points, you can prevent it from dying, causing it to gain the Unconscious condition instead, as if it had been knocked out (no action required).
**Kill**  
***Deadly Strike.*** Once per turn when you deal damage to a creature with your *Hunter’s Mark*, the spell’s damage increases by 1d6.  
***No Escape.*** While the creature marked by your *Hunter’s Mark* is Bloodied, you always know its precise location, provided it is within 1 mile of you, and its Speed is reduced by 10 feet.

## Level 3: Expert Bindings
Over the course of one minute, you can tie and bind a Large or smaller creature within 5 feet of you that is willing or has the Incapacitated condition. The material required to tie up the creature depends on its size: 1 foot of String if it’s Tiny, 15 feet of Rope if it’s Small or Medium, and 30 feet of Rope if it’s Large.  
A creature bound in this way has the Restrained and Incapacitated conditions until it is untied. A creature within 5 feet of it can take an action to free the target by making a successful Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check against your spell save DC.

## Level 7: Hunter’s Arsenal
You can cast one of the spells you have prepared with your Bounty Hunter Spells feature without expending a spell slot. Once you cast a spell this way, you can’t do so again until you finish a Long Rest.  
In addition, you are able to concentrate on one of these spells at the same time as concentrating on *Hunter’s Mark*. If you need to roll to maintain Concentration, roll for each spell separately.  
Starting at Ranger level 15, this benefit extends to any Ranger spell you have prepared, not just your Bounty Hunter spells.

## Level 11: Enhanced Tactics
You can deploy skilled tactics against your quarry, enhancing your intent to kill or capture it.  
You can use one of these benefits a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once) and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest. Alternatively, you can expend a level 1+ spell slot to use it again.  
***Restraining Strike.*** When a creature fails its saving throw against your Restricting Strike, you can also give it the Restrained condition, which lasts until the Grappled condition ends on it.  
***Lethal Strike.*** When you deal your Deadly Strike damage, you can increase the damage of *Hunter’s Mark* by a further 2d6.

## Level 15: Sole Focus
When you target a creature with your *Hunter’s Mark*, or when you start your turn with it active, you can choose to become hyper-aware of the marked creature and its tactics. Until the start of your next turn, you have Advantage on saving throws you make against its spells and effects, and Resistance to any damage it deals to you.  
This awareness comes with a cost: until the start of your next turn, you have Disadvantage on attack rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks you make against any other creature.
