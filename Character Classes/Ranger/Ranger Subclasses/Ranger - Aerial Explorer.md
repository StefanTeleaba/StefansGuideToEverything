# Aerial Explorer
*Take to the Air and Explore the Skies*  
A common fantasy of ground dwelling creatures is to take to the skies - an act of absolute freedom. You are one such dreamer, and have practiced the magic of manipulating air and wind to achieve this goal. With the aid of a piece of specialised gliding equipment, the skies are now gleefully yours to explore.

## Level 3: Aeromancy Magic
Your practice in Aeromancy ensures you always have certain spells ready; when you reach a Ranger level specified in the Aeromancy Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.  
**Aeromancy Spells**
| Ranger Level | Spells      |
|:------------:|-------------|
| 3            | Longstrider |
| 5            | Gust of Wind |
| 9            | Wind Wall |
| 13           | Freedom of Movement |
| 17           | Conjure Elemental (Air only) |

## Level 3: Glider
You have constructed, found or inherited a gliding apparatus which can keep you aloft with the aid of aeromancy. The glider has an appearance of your choice, though your choice doesn’t affect its game statistics.  
At any time while you are more than 10 feet off the ground and are falling, you can take a Reaction to begin Gliding until you reach the ground or you have the Incapacitated condition. If you stop Gliding while in the air, you fall. Your glider can support little more than your own weight; you cannot Glide while you are encumbered or while you are wearing Heavy Armor. You also cannot Glide while you are Grappling a creature, or have a Speed of 0. Gliding imparts the following properties on you:  
***Descent.*** You descend 5 feet at the start of each of your turns, and again each time you make an attack. You ignore this property if you have a Fly Speed.  
***Movement.*** On your turn you can move through the air as if you had a Fly Speed equal to your Speed, but you cannot ascend. You ignore this property if you have a Fly Speed.  
***Volitant Strike.*** Once per turn when you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can propel the strike with bursts of wind, causing it to deal an extra 1d6 damage. This damage increases to 2d6 when you reach level 11.

## Level 3: Updraft
As a Bonus Action, you can call on elemental wind to launch yourself up into the air. When you do so, you ascend 30 feet. You can halt this movement at any time.  
The distance you ascend increases when you reach certain levels in this class: to 45 feet at level 7, and to 60 feet at level 11.  
You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum once). You regain one expended use when you finish a Short Rest, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

## Level 7: Evasive Maneuver
You can deftly maneuver your glider out of the way of incoming hazards. While you are Gliding, if you are targeted by an attack or are forced to make a saving throw by an area of effect, you can take a Reaction to maneuver out of the way, moving 15 feet in a direction of your choice without provoking Opportunity Attacks. One of the following effects occurs, depending on what you were targeted with. Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest. Alternatively, you can expend a use of your Updraft feature to use it.  
***Attack.*** If this movement causes you to move out of range of the attack, it automatically misses. Otherwise, the attack is made against you at Disadvantage.  
***Area of Effect.*** If this movement causes you to entirely move out of the area, you are unaffected by it. Otherwise you have Advantage on any Strength or Dexterity saving throw you make to resist the effect on this turn.

## Level 11: Soaring
Your skills in aeromancy have grown tremendously, improving your Glider feature in the following ways.  
***Movement.*** While Gliding, your Speed increases by 10 feet and you can now move freely in any direction, including upwards.  
***Maneuverability.*** While Gliding, you can Disengage as a Bonus Action, which can be part of the same Bonus Action you use to cast or move your *Hunter’s Mark*.  
***Volitant Strike.*** When you use your Volitant Strike, you can additionally push the target 10 feet directly away from you, provided the target is no more than one size larger than you. If the target is on the ground, it is pushed horizontally in a direction of your choice.

## Level 15: Aerial Dominance
You now regain all expended uses of your Updraft feature when you finish a Short Rest.  
In addition, when you use Updraft, you can now launch yourself in any direction of your choice, not just upwards, and you can maneuver through the spaces of other creatures and objects unimpeded (but not through walls or structures).
