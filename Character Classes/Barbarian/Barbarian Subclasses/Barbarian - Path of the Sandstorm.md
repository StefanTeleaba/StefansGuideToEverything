# Path of the Sandstorm
*Destroy your foes with the power of the desert.*  
Barbarians who follow the Path of the Sandstorm learn how to channel the primal forces of the desert, becoming one with the endless sands.  
These warriors become living sandstorms, slipping past their opponents defences, engulfing foes in swirling sands, and carrying enemies away on scorching desert winds.

## Level 3: Desert Dweller
Your body is as accustomed to the desert's heat as it is to its sand. You gain resistance to Fire damage.

## Level 3: Living Sand
While your Rage is active, you can take a Bonus Action to transform yourself into desert sand. This transformation lasts until the end of your turn. You can also end this transformation early (no action required).  
While transformed, you gain the following benefits.  
***Primal Connection.*** If you transform while in an area of natural sand, doing so extends your Rage until the end of your next turn.  
***Sand Form.*** You can move through the space of another creature, and through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide. Your equipment can't pass through a space with you unless it is small enough to fit through it. If your Living Sand ends with you in another creature’s space, you take 5 force damage and are moved to the nearest unoccupied space.  
***Scouring Sands.*** When you enter the space of another creature on your turn, you can deal Slashing damage to that creature equal to your Rage Damage bonus. You cannot deal this damage to a creature more than once on a turn.
***Untouchable.*** Opportunity Attacks against you have Disadvantage.

## Level 6: Sand Tempest
Your primal connection to the desert grows, allowing you to carry your foes away in scorching desert winds. When you deal damage with Scouring Sand, you can immediately attempt to Grapple the target (no action required). A creature Grappled in this way doesn't reduce your Speed when you move it. The grapple automatically ends when your Living Sand ends, and the creature appears in an unoccupied space of your choice within 5 feet of you.  
Additionally, you can choose to deal Force damage with your Scouring Sand.

## Level 10: Greater Sandstorm
Your sandstorm grows even more imposing, granting your Living Sand the following benefits.  
***Sand Form.*** When you use Living Sand, you can become Large.  
Additionally, you can Grapple a number of creatures equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1) while transformed by Living Sand.  
Finally, your worn and carried equipment can be transformed into sand with you. You and your possessions can move through any opening large enough for a grain of sand to pass through.  
***Scouring Sands.*** When you deal damage with this feature, you gain an additional 10 feet of movement until the end of your turn, up to a maximum equal to your Movement Speed.  
***Untouchable.*** Opportunity Attacks against you automatically miss.

## Level 14: Endless Sandstorm
Your transformation now truly embodies the vast and unforgiving desert. When you use Living Sand, you can become Huge.  
Additionally, when you use Living Sand, you can remain transformed until the end of your next turn.
