# Path of the Mummy
*Never Let Them Go*  
Barbarians who walk the Path of the Mummy contain a rage so ancient and powerful that it threatens to tear their bodies apart. As such, they find themselves surrounded by spectral linens when channeling this ancient strength, which serve not only as methods to keep themselves from falling apart, but also as hungering extensions of their own will.

## Level 3: Clutching Linens
*The wrappings that bind your ancient rage are not merely decorative- they are hungry. Grave cloths lash out on your command, coiling around flesh and bone alike, eager to drag the living toward the stillness of the tomb.*  
***Clutching Linens:*** When you activate your rage or as a Bonus Action while your Rage is active, you can cause a flowing tendril of spectral linen to lash out at a Large or Smaller creature within 10 feet of you. Make an attack roll against that creature. On a hit, that creature has the Grappled Condition. You can have a number of creatures grappled this way at a time equal to your Rage Damage Bonus.  
***Desert’s Wrath:*** When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can replace one of your attacks with one of the following effects against a creature you have grappled with your Clutching Linens:  
**Drain:** The creature must make a Constitution Saving Throw (DC = 8 + Proficiency + Strength Modifier) or take Necrotic Damage. To determine the Necrotic damage, roll a number of d8s equal to your Rage Damage Bonus, and add them together. On a successful save, the creature takes half damage only.  
**Reposition:** You can move the creature to an unoccupied space within 10 feet of yourself.

## Level 6: Rites of Preservation
*The forces of unmaking bend to your will, inuring you against the forces of death as your linens preserve not only you, but ensure that your foes will remain trapped with you for all time.*  
***Embalmed:*** You gain resistance to Necrotic Damage.  
***Tightening Coils:*** Your Clutching Linens can grapple creatures two sizes larger than you. Additionally, when targeting a creature with your Clutching Linens, if the attack roll exceeds the target's AC by 5 or more, they also become Restrained while they are Grappled by you. If the attack is a critical hit, you can immediately use Drain on the target (no action required).

## Level 10: Creeping Decay
*The hunger of the force of unmaking that flows through your wrappings increases in intensity, expediting your foes journey into the realm of the dead.*  
Creatures that end their turns grappled by your Clutching Linens take Necrotic Damage equal to your Constitution Modifier plus half your Barbarian Level. A creature that takes damage in this way cannot regain hit points until the end of their next turn.

## Level 14: Whirlwind of Sand
*Woe to those who dare to strike you, for they may find themselves dragged away into the silence of the tomb.*  
As a reaction when you are hit, you can add your Constitution Modifier to your AC, potentially causing the triggering attack to miss. You can then teleport up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. Creatures grappled by your Clutching Linens travel with you, arriving in unoccupied spaces of your choice within 10 feet of you. Each creature of your choice within 10 feet of your Destination space additionally must succeed on a Constitution Saving Throw (DC = 8 + Proficiency Bonus + Constitution) or have the Blinded Condition until the end of your next turn.  
You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Constitution Modifier, and regain all uses at the end of a Long Rest.
