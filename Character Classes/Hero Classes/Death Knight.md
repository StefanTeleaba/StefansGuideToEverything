# Death Knight
**Playing a Death Knight**  
Death knight is a hero class, which means you typically must start play at level 3 or higher (see the "Hero Classes" sidebar). To play a death knight, choose the Paladin class from the Player's Handbook and the Oath of the Ebon Blade subclass, described below. This subclass alters some of your core Paladin class features beginning at level 1.  
As a death knight, you can choose one of three specializations by inscribing the corresponding rune on your weapon using your Runeforging feature: Blood, Frost, or Unholy.  
**Blood Specialization**  
The following choices will help you focus on this specialization:  
***Level 3.*** Choose Greataxes, Greatswords, or Mauls for your Weapon Mastery feature. Choose Interception for a Fighting Style feat.  
***Feats.*** Consider the following feats at higher levels: Charger, Crusher, Great Weapon Fighting, Great Weapon Master, Heavy Armor Master, Piercer, Slasher, and Tough.  
**Frost Specialization**  
Wielding the Frost rune, you are an icy harbinger of doom, channeling runic power and delivering vicious weapon strikes. The following choices will help you focus on this specialization:
***Level 3.*** Choose Light Hammers or Scimitars for your Weapon Mastery feature. Choose Two-Weapon Fighting got a Fighting Style feat.  
***Feats.*** Consider the following feats at higher levels: Charger, Crusher, Dual Wielder, and Slasher.  
**Unholy Specialization**  
With the Unholy rune, you become a master of death and decay, spreading infection and controlling undead minions. The following choices will help you focus on this specialization:  
***Level 3.*** Choose Greataxes, Greatswords, or Mauls for your Weapon Mastery feature. Choose Great Weapon Fighting for a Fighting Style feat.  
***Feats.*** Consider the following feats at higher levels: Charger, Crusher, Great Weapon Master, Piercer, and Slasher.

## Level 1: Death Coil
This feature replaces the Lay On Hands feature of the Paladin class.  
Your creature type becomes Undead.  
You also gain the ability to manipulate negative energy. You have a pool of d8s to fuel this ability. The number of dice in the pool equals one plus your Charisma modifier (minimum of one die).  
As a Bonus Action, you can target a creature you can see within 60 feet of yourself and force it to make a Constitution saving throw, expending dice from the pool. The maximum number of dice you can expend at once equals your Charisma modifier (minimum of one die). Roll the dice you expend. The target takes Necrotic damage equal to the roll's total on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. If the target is an Undead, you can choose to have it regain Hit Points equal to the roll's total instead. Your pool regains all expended dice when you finish a Long Rest.

## Level 2: Runic Smite
This feature replaces the Paladin's Smite feature of the Paladin class.  
You always have the *Rune Strike* spell (see "Death Knight Spells") prepared. You can cast it once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a Long Rest.

## Level 5: Faithful Steed
In addition to the benefits of the Paladin's Faithful Steed feature, when you cast *Find Steed*, the **Otherworldly Steed** can be Undead. An Undead steed deals Necrotic damage with its Otherworldly Slam action and has this Bonus Action:  
***Ghostly Stride (Undead Only; Recharges After a Long Rest).*** The steed and its rider can move through other creatures and objects this turn as if they were Difficult Terrain. Both steed and rider take 5 (1d10) Force dam- age if they end their turn inside an object.

## Level 11: Runic Strikes
This feature replaces the Paladin's Radiant Strikes feature.  
When you hit a target with an attack roll using a Melee weapon or an Unarmed Strike, the target takes an extra 1d8 Necrotic damage.

## Level 14: Improved Death Coil
This feature replaces the Paladin's Restoring Touch feature.  
When you use Death Coil to deal damage, the target takes extra Necrotic damage equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of 1) whether or not it fais its saving throw. If the target is an Undead, and you have it regain Hit Points, it regains additional Hit Points equal to your Paladin level.
