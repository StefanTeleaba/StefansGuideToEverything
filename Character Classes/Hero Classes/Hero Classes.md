# Hero Classes
Three of the World of Warcraft classes—death knight, demon hunter, and evoker—are hero classes, meaning that they are typically available only to characters starting at level 3 or higher. In the story of World of Warcraft, characters with these classes are already experienced and powerful adventurers by the time they enter the game. The death knight and the demon hunter classes are represented by subclasses (the Oath of the Ebon Blade Paladin and the Demon Hunter Ranger, respectively) that alter the character's base class. With your DM's permission, you can start as a level 1 character of one of these classes, but your subclass choice is fixed when you make the character.  
Playing an evoker doesn't alter the base Sorcerer class, but it slightly alters the Draconic Sorcery subclass.
