# The Djinn
*Strike from mist and unleash the fury of the sands*  
Djinn rogues have trained extensively in the harshest of desert climates, drawing their power from the elemental spirits which call the sands home. Rogues trained in such deserts can befuddle their enemies with mirages and disappear into a cloud of sand, only to reappear elsewhere with a deadly sneak attack.

## Level 3: Sandstrider
Immediately after you deal your Sneak Attack damage on your turn, you can become incorporeal (no action required), as you disappear into a cloud of sand and swirling, colorless smoke. Until the end of your turn, Opportunity Attacks against you have Disadvantage and you can move through other creatures' space without a movement penalty. If you would end your turn in the space of another creature, you instead appear in the nearest unoccupied space.  
Additionally, if you take the Hide action while in your sand form, you do not need to be Heavily Obscured or benefitting from Cover to do so. Instead, you remain as a wisp of sand and smoke until you are no longer Hidden or the end of your next turn, whichever comes first. You do not benefit from the effects of being in your sand form for this duration.

## Level 9: Mirage
You gain the following Cunning Strike option:  
***Mirage (Cost 3d6).*** The target makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it sees an illusory paradise at a point of your choice within 60 feet which you and the target can see. On its next turn, it must use its full movement speed to reach the location, potentially provoking Opportunity Attacks. If it reaches the location on its turn, it falls Prone and ends its turn as it attempts to eat or drink the magnificent food and beverages it sees in this illusion. You can't target a location the creature would be unable to reach with its movement speed(s), or one which would cause it direct harm or death, such as one in midair, in the middle of a body of water, or off a sheer drop.

## Level 9: Shamshir
One-handed melee weapons with the Nick property (Dagger, Sickle, Light Hammer, Scimitar) also gain the Vex weapon mastery for you. Similarly, the Shortsword, Rapier and Handaxe gain the Nick weapon mastery for you. When you use one of these weapons, you can choose which Mastery property to use with a weapon when you take Attack action, but before you make an attack roll.

## Level 13: Whirlwind
When you use your Sandstrider feature to enter your sand form, you can empower yourself with the fury of a swirling sandstorm. Until the end of your turn, whenever you move through another creature's space while in your sand form, you can make one melee weapon attack against that creature (no action required). A creature can’t be targeted with more than one attack in this way.  
Once you use Whirlwind, you can't do so again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

## Level 13: Weightless
You can take a Reaction when you fall to reduce any damage you take from the fall by an amount equal to two times your Rogue level. Additionally, while in your sand form, you ignore the effects of Difficult Terrain.

## Level 17: Caliph
Your connection to the Djinn and sands grants you great control over your elemental abilities. You gain the following benefits:  
***Sandstorm.*** You can use Whirlwind one additional time before you finish a Short or Long Rest. On your turn while in your sand form, you have Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing and Slashing damage.  
***Scouring Sand.*** When you deal your Sneak Attack damage to a creature, that damage ignores a creature’s Resistance to your Sneak Attack’s damage type, and treats damage Immunity as Resistance.  
***Empowered Illusions.*** Your Mirage now causes the target great distress once it learns of the deception. When a creature falls Prone as a result of Mirage, it takes 3d6 Psychic damage.
