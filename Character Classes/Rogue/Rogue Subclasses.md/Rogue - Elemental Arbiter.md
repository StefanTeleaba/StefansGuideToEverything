# Elemental Striker
*Infuse Weapon Strikes with Elemental Power*  
Elemental Strikers are thieves of nature’s power, channeling fire’s fury, water’s grace, earth’s strength, and air’s swiftness. They wield these stolen gifts with style, turning every skirmish into a dazzling dance of elemental chaos.

## Level 3: Elemental Artifact
You have found or created an artifact capable of storing elemental energy. The artifact is a Tiny object with an appearance of your choice that can fit within one hand. The artifact has the following properties:  
***Known Elements.*** Select two elements from Air, Earth, Fire and Water. These are your known elements. You can change your selections when you finish a Long Rest. Starting at level 9 you can select three options, and at level 13 you gain all four.  
***Elemental DC.*** Effects produced by the artifact use your Elemental DC, which is equal to 8 plus your Proficiency Bonus and your Dexterity modifier.  
***Restoring the Artifact.*** If you lose the artifact or if it is destroyed, you can gain a new one by communing with the elements over the course of a Long Rest.  
***Energy Infusion.*** When you use Sneak Attack while the artifact is on your person, you can change its damage type to match one of your Known Elements, as shown on the Elemental Damage Types table.  
**Elemental Damage Types**
| Element | Damage Type |
|---------|-------------|
| Air     | Thunder     |
| Earth   | Bludgeoning |
| Fire    | Fire        |
| Water   | Cold        |

## Level 3: Empowerment of the Elements
While your Elemental Artifact is on your person, you can use it to enhance your other features with elemental effects, depending on your Known Elements:  
***Air.*** When you use your Cunning Action to Dash, you can boost your movement with a tailwind of rushing air. Your Speed increases by 10 feet for that action, and until the end of your turn you can move along vertical and liquid surfaces without falling.  
***Earth.*** When you use your Steady Aim feature you can draw earth to your body, keeping you steady and protecting you. You gain Temporary Hit Points equal to half your Rogue level (round up), which last until the start of your next turn. While you have these Temporary Hit Points, you cannot be moved against your will or be inflicted with the Prone condition.  
***Fire.*** When you use your Cunning Action to Disengage, you create an instantaneous burst of flame as you move. Each creature within 5 feet of you must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw against your Elemental DC or take Fire damage equal to 1d6 plus half your Rogue level (round up).  
***Water.*** When you use your Cunning Action to Hide, you can use the air’s moisture to create a misty cloud. The cloud appears in a 10 foot Emanation around you, its area is Lightly Obscured and you can Hide within it. It remains in the space you created it until the end of your next turn, and it disperses early if it is subjected to a strong wind.

## Level 9: Infused Strikes
When you use Energy Infusion to change your Sneak Attack’s damage type, you gain access to an additional Cunning Strike option, depending on your choice of damage type.  
***Bludgeoning (Cost: 2d6).*** The ground in a 10 foot radius around the target becomes Difficult Terrain for 1 minute or until you use this option again. The target must be on the ground when you use this option.  
***Cold (Cost: 2d6).*** The target makes a Strength saving throw. On a failure, it has the Restrained condition until the start of your next turn.  
***Fire (Cost: 1d6).*** The target makes a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, you cause the target to catch on fire for 1 minute. The target takes 2d6 Fire damage at the start of each of its turns while on fire. The flame persists until a creature within 5 feet of the target takes an action to douse the flame, or it completely enters a region the flame cannot burn, such as a body of water.  
***Thunder (Cost: 1d6).*** The target makes a Strength saving throw. On a failure, it is pushed 15 feet straight away from you by an intense current of air.

## Level 13: Conflux
You gain the following benefits.  
***Mixed Elements.*** When you use Energy Infusion to change your Sneak Attack’s damage type, you can choose to divide the damage dice as you wish between Bludgeoning, Cold, Fire and Thunder. If you use Cunning Strike and only apply Infused Strikes options, you can apply a third one. Starting at level 17, you can apply all four.  
***Elemental Casting.*** You can cast the following spells without the need for components or expending a spell slot: Control Water, Fire Shield, Fly and Stone Shape. Dexterity is your spellcasting ability for these spells. Once you cast a spell in this way, you can’t cast it or any of the other options again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

## Level 17: Elemental Assault
When you use Energy Infusion to change your Sneak Attack’s damage type, each creature of your choice in a 10 foot Emanation around the target, other than the target itself, must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Elemental DC. On a failure, a creature takes 3d6 damage of one of the damage types you chose as it is assailed by earth, water, fire or air. On a success, it takes half as much damage.
