# Ward of the Sheltering Hands
A turn of ill luck led you to the Sheltering Hands, an altruistic organization dedicated to defending the downtrodden. The organization helped you regain your footing and taught you some of its protective magic - in the hope that one day you too would shelter the less fortunate in their time of need.

**Ability Scores:** Constitution, Wisdom, Charisma  
**Feat:** Arcane Safeguard  
**Skill Proficiencies:** Insight and Medicine  
**Tool Proficiency:** Cook’s Utensils  
**Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) Cook’s Utensils, Blanket, Healer’s Kit, Lamp, Oil (3 flasks), Tinderbox, Traveler’s Clothes, Waterskin, 40 GP; or (B) 50 GP
