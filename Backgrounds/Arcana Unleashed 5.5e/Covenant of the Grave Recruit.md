# Covenant of the Grave Recruit
Death has always fascinated you. You’ve studied that morbid topic alongside members of the Covenant of the Grave, analyzing the macabre with scientific precision. Though these studies, you’ve amassed knowledge of medication, necromancy, and history.

**Ability Scores:** Strength, Intelligence, Wisdom  
**Feat:** Arcane Undertaker  
**Skill Proficiencies:** History and Medicine  
**Tool Proficiency:** Herbalism Kit  
**Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) Dagger, Herbalism Kit, Book (anatomy), Shovel, 16 GP; or (B) 50 GP
