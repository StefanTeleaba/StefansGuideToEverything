# Crucible Storm Chaser
Your daredevil tendencies and thirst for excitement led you to the Crucible Keepers, an organization dedicated to studying natural and magical catastrophes. From playing with molten glass to charging into a hurricane’s eye, you thrive when disaster is mere steps away.

**Ability Scores:** Strength, Constitution, Intelligence  
**Feat:** Arcane Overload  
**Skill Proficiencies:** Athletics and Nature  
**Tool Proficiency:** Glassblower’s Tools  
**Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) Glassblower’s Tools, Backpack, Rope, Traveler’s Clothes, 15 GP; or (B) 50 GP
