# Cosmic Dawn Experiment
Your body has been warped by the transmutation practices of the Bringers of the Cosmic Dawn. Perhaps you were inadvertently caught up in one of the organization’s experiments, or maybe you were a member of the organization and sought to improve your physical form through magical might. Whatever the case, you use your strange anatomy to your benefit.

**Ability Scores:** Strength, Dexterity, Constitution  
**Feat:** Transmuted Anatomy  
**Skill Proficiencies:** Athletics and Survival  
**Tool Proficiency:** Choose one kind of Artisan’s Tools  
**Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) Artisan’s Tools (same as above), Backpack, Robe, Traveler’s Clothes, 30 GP; or (B) 50 GP
