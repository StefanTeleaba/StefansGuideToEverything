# Seer Apprentice
As an apprentice among the Seers of Sea and Sky, you learned to interpret minor omens from the clouds above and the fathoms below. The organization’s rich network of libraries furthered your education, and you now travel across the world, imparting advice and aid to others.

**Ability Scores:** Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma  
**Feat:** Arcane Omens  
**Skill Proficiencies:** History and Insight  
**Tool Proficiency:** Navigator’s Tools  
**Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) Navigator’s Tools, 8 Candles, Ink, Ink Pen, Parchment (9 sheets), Pouch, Tinderbox, Traveler’s Clothes, 11 GP; or (B) 50 GP
