# Horizon Weaver Initiate
Your adolescence was marked by unquenchable wanderlust. You left home and began your travels as soon as you were able. Along the way, you encountered the adventurous conjurers of the Horizon Weavers, who taught you some of their teleportation magic to further your explorations.

**Ability Scores:** Dexterity, Constitution, Wisdom  
**Feat:** Portal Jumper  
**Skill Proficiencies:** Acrobatics and Survival  
**Tool Proficiency:** Weaver’s Tools  
**Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) Shortbow, 20 Arrows, Weaver’s Tools, Map, Quiver, Rope, Traveler’s Clothes, 18 GP; or (B) 50 GP
