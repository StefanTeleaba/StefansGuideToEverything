# Phantasmic Circus Trouper
Whether as a performer or a circus hand, you found a home among the members of the Phantasmic Circus. As you traveled across the world bringing magic and wonder to excited audiences, you learned some illusion magic and honed your talents for performance and misdirection.

**Ability Scores:** Dexterity, Constitution, Charisma  
**Feat:** Arcane Artist  
**Skill Proficiencies:** Deception and Performance  
**Tool Proficiency:** Disguise Kit  
**Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) Disguise Kit, Gaming Set (any), Costume, Mirror, Traveler’s Clothes, 12 GP; or (B) 50 GP
