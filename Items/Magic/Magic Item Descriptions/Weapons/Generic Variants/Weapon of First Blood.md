*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Weapon of First Blood
*Weapon (Any Weapon), Uncommon*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit a creature with this weapon, the weapon deals an extra 1d8 damage to the target if it isn't missing any of its Hit Points.

*Weapon (Any Weapon), Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit a creature with this weapon, the weapon deals an extra 1d8 damage to the target if it isn't missing any of its Hit Points.
While you are attuned to this magic weapon, you have Advantage on Initiative rolls. During the first round of each combat, you have Advantage on attack rolls against any creature that hasn't taken a turn. If you hit any target during that round, the target takes an extra 1d8 damage of the weapon's type.
