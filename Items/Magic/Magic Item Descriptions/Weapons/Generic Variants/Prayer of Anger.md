*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Prayer of Anger
*Weapon (Claymore, Falchion, Longsword, or Greatsword), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. While you are attuned to this weapon, you have Immunity to the Charmed and Frightened conditions, you gain a +5 bonus to Strength checks and Strength saving throws, and your Rage Damage bonus is doubled. In addition, when you're hit by an attack while you're wielding this weapon, any Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage dealt to you by that attack is reduced by 5.

---

*The sword was inscribed with the holy symbol of Tempus, god of battle.*
