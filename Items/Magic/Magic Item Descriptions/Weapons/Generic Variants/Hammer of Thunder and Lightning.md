*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Hammer of Thunder and Lightning
*Weapon (Light Hammer, Maul, or Warhammer), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. It deals an extra 1d8 Thunder damage to any creature it hits. It also has the following additional properties. Once one of these properties is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.  
***Lightning.*** When you hit a target, you can cause it to take an extra 2d8 Lightning damage (no action required).  
***Thunder.*** When you hit a creature, you can cause the weapon to emit a crack of thunder audible out to 300 feet (no action required). The target you hit must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or have the Stunned condition until the end of your next turn.  
***Thunder and Lightning.*** Immediately after you hit a creature, you can take a Bonus Action to use the ***Lightning*** and ***Thunder*** properties (see above) at the same time. Doing so doesn't expend the daily use of those properties, only the use of this one.  
***Lightning Strike.*** You can take a Magic action to cause a bolt of lightning to leap from the weapon in a Line that is 5 feet wide and 120 feet long. Each creature in that Line makes a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 9d8 Lightning damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.  
***Thunderclap.*** You can take a Magic action to cause the weapon to produce a thunderclap audible out to 600 feet. Every creature within a 60-foot Emanation originating from you makes a DC 17 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 2d8 Thunder damage and has the Deafened condition for 1 minute. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only.
