*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Mithral Weapon
*Weapon (Any Ammunition or Melee Weapon), Uncommon*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. This magic weapon has the Light property.

*Weapon (Any Ammunition or Melee Weapon), Rare*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. This magic weapon has the Light property and its Critical Range increases by 1.

*Weapon (Any Ammunition or Melee Weapon), Very Rare*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. This magic weapon has the Light property and its Critical Range increases by 1. When you attack with this magic weapon, you can activate the Nick property in addition to a different mastery property you’re using with this weapon.

*Weapon (Any Ammunition or Melee Weapon), Legendary*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. This magic weapon has the Light property and its Critical Range increases by 1. When you attack with this magic weapon, you can activate the Nick property in addition to a different mastery property you’re using with this weapon. When you take the Attack action on your turn and attack with this magic weapon, you can make one extra attack as part of that action. That extra attack must be made with the same weapon.
