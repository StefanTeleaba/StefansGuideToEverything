*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Master's Riposte
*Weapon (Longsword or Rapier), Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.  
***Riposte.*** When a creature misses you with a melee attack roll, you can take a Reaction to make a melee attack roll with the sword against the creature.

*Weapon (Longsword or Rapier), Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.  
***Riposte.*** When a creature targets you with a melee attack roll, you can take a Reaction to make a melee attack roll with the sword against the creature.

*Weapon (Longsword or Rapier), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.  
***Riposte.*** When a creature misses you with a melee attack roll, you can take a Reaction to make a melee attack roll with the sword against the creature.  
***Parry.*** When a creature hits you with a melee attack, you can take a Reaction to add your Proficiency Bonus to your Armor Class. You gain this bonus to your AC against melee attacks until the start of your next turn. You can make a melee attack roll with the sword against the attacker as part of this Reaction.
