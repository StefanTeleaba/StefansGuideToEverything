*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Woundseeker
*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Uncommon*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.  
***Deepen the Wound.*** This weapon deals an additional 1d6 damage to the target if it's missing any of its Hit Points.

*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Rare*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.  
***Deepen the Wound.*** This weapon deals an additional 1d6 damage to the target if it's missing any of its Hit Points or and additional 2d6 damage if the target is Bloodied.

*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Very Rare*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.  
***Deepen the Wound.*** This weapon deals an additional 1d6 damage to the target if it's missing any of its Hit Points or and additional 2d6 damage if the target is Bloodied. In addition, when you score a Critical Hit, that target takes an extra 3d6 damage if it is Bloodied.
