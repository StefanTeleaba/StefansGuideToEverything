*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Sword of the Bat
*Weapon (Greatsword, Longsword, Rapier, or Shortsword), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. As long as this weapon is within your reach and you are attuned to it, you have Blindsight with a range of 30 feet. If you already have Blindsight, the weapon increases its range by 30 feet.  
***Blade Bite.*** Immediately after hittiing a creature with a Melee weapon or an Unarmed Strike, you can take a Bonus Action to expend one Hit Die and siphon life from the target. The target takes an extra 3d6 Necrotic damage, and you regain Hit Points equal to half the amount of Necrotic damage dealt.  
You can use the weapon to cast the *Find Familiar (Bat form only)* spell as a Magic action without expending a spell slot. When you take the Attack action, you can forgo one of your own attacks to allow your familiar to make one attack of its own with its Reaction. If it hits, you can use the *Blade Bite* feature through the familiar. You can also command the familiar to take the Attack action as a Bonus Action.

*Weapon (Greatsword, Longsword, Rapier, or Shortsword), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. As long as this weapon is within your reach and you are attuned to it, you have Blindsight with a range of 60 feet. If you already have Blindsight, the weapon increases its range by 60 feet.  
***Blade Bite.*** Immediately after hittiing a creature with a Melee weapon or an Unarmed Strike, you can take a Bonus Action to expend one Hit Die and siphon life from the target. The target takes an extra 3d6 Necrotic damage, and you regain Hit Points equal to half the amount of Necrotic damage dealt.  
You can use the weapon to cast the *Find Familiar (Bat or Giant Bat)* spell as a Magic action without expending a spell slot. When you take the Attack action, you can forgo one of your own attacks to allow your familiar to make one attack of its own with its Reaction. If it hits, you can use the *Blade Bite* feature through the familiar. You can also command the familiar to take the Attack action as a Bonus Action.
