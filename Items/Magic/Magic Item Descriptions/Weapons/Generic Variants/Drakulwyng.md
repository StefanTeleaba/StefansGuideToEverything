*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Drakulwyng
*Weapon (Dagger, Longsword, Rapier, Scimitar, or Shortsword), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. In addiiton, as long as you are attuned to it, your Strength, Constitution and Charisma scores increase by 2, to a maximum of 24.  
When you hit a creature with an attack, the target takes an extra 3d6 Necrotic damage, and you regain Hit Points equal to the amount of Necrotic damage dealt. If you have all of your Hit Points, you instead gain a number of Temporary Hit Points equal to the amount of Necrotic damage dealt. When you score a Critical Hit, the target takes an additional 4d6 Necrotic damage.
