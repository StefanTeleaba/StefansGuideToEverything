*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Storm Bow
*Weapon (Longbow or Shortbow), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit with an attack roll using this magic weapon, the target takes an extra 2d6 Lightning damage.  
***Storm Shell.*** As a Bonus Action, you can summon a shell of storms that surrounds your body for 1 minute. The shell grants you Resistance to Cold, Fire and Lightning damage. When you first summon the shell and as a Bonus Action on each of your later turns until the effect ends, you can cause a bolt of lightning to leap to a creature of your choice within 30 feet of you. The target makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d6 Lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. In addition, whenever a creature hits you with a melee attack roll while the shell is active, the attacker takes 4d6 Lightning damage. Once this property is used, it can’t be used again until the next dawn.
