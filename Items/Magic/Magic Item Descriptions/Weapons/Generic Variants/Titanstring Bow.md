*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Titanstring Bow
*Weapon (Longbow or Shortbow), Uncommon*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon and you can add your Strength modifier to the damage rolls.

*Weapon (Longbow or Shortbow), Rare*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon and you can add your Strength modifier to the damage rolls.

*Weapon (Longbow or Shortbow), Very Rare*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon and you can add your Strength modifier to the damage rolls.
