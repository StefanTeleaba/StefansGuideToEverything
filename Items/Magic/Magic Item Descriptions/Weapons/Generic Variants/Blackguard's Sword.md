*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Blackguard's Sword
*Weapon (Greatsword or Longsword), Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*  
This magic weapon deals an additional 1d8 Necrotic damage on a Hit.  
***Dazing Smite.*** Immediately after you cast *Divine Smite*, the target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target has the Stunned condition until the start of your next turn.

*Weapon (Greatsword or Longsword), Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit with an attack roll using this magic weapon, the target takes an extra 1d8 Necrotic damage.  
***Dazing Smite.*** Immediately after you cast *Divine Smite*, the target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target has the Stunned condition until the start of your next turn.

*Weapon (Greatsword or Longsword), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit with an attack roll using this magic weapon, the target takes an extra 1d8 Necrotic damage.  
***Dazing Smite.*** Immediately after you cast *Divine Smite*, the target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target has the Stunned condition until the start of your next turn.

*Weapon (Greatsword or Longsword), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit with an attack roll using this magic weapon, the target takes an extra 2d8 Necrotic damage.  
***Dazing Smite.*** Immediately after you cast *Divine Smite*, the target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target has the Stunned condition until the start of your next turn.

---

*Formerly wielded by one who forsook all ties of friendship and kindness, this sword is rife with deranged energies. It seems to transmit feelings of contentment when carving through bones.*
