*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Cutter
*Weapon (Claymore, Falchion, Greatsword, or Longsword), Legendary*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon and its Critical Range increases by 1. If you hit a creature with a melee attack roll using this weapon, you can make a melee attack roll with the weapon against a second creature within 5 feet of the first that is also within your reach. You can make this extra attack only once per turn.
