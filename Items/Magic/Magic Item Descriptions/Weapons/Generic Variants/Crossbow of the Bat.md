# Acid-Etched Crossbow
*Weapon (Hand Crossbow, Heavy Crossbow, or Light Crossbow), Uncommon*  
When you attack with this weapon while entirely in Dim Light or Darkness, the range of this weapon is doubled.
***Summon Swarm.*** When you hit a creature with this weapon, you can use a command word to cause a **Swarm of Bats** to appear in the target's space. For 1 minute or until it is reduced to 0 Hit Points, the swarm moves with the target and attacks it at the start of each of the target's turns. If a creature uses teleportation, the swarm disappears. Once this property is used, it can’t be used again until the next dusk.

# Crossbow of the Bat
*Weapon (Hand Crossbow, Heavy Crossbow, or Light Crossbow), Uncommon*  
Your normal range with this weapon is doubled if it's fired from dim light or darkness.  
***Summon Swarm.*** You can speak the weapon's command word as part of firing a crossbow bolt from it. On a hit, a swarm of bats appears in the same space as the target of the attack. The swarm remains for 1 minute or until it's reduced to 0 hit points. For the duration, it remains in the target's space (following it if it moves) and attacks with its Bite at the start of each of the target's turns. It otherwise takes no turns. A creature that teleports away from the swarm or uses a similar effect causes the swarm to disappear. Once the swarm appears, this property can't be used again until the next dusk.  
*From the darkness I strike. Silent, but all-seeing.*
