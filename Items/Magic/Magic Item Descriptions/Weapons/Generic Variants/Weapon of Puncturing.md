*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Weapon of Puncturing
*Weapon (Any Piercing Weapon), Rare*  
When you attack a creature with this weapon and score a Critical Hit, that target takes an extra 4d6 Piercing damage.

*Weapon (Any Piercing Weapon), Very Rare*  
When you attack a creature with this weapon and score a Critical Hit, that target takes an extra 6d6 Piercing damage.
