*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Rhythm Echo
*Weapon (Falchion, Greatsword, or Longsword), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon, its Critical Range increases by 1, and it ignores Resistance to Slashing damage. When you score a Critical Hit with this weapon, the target takes an additional 30 Slashing damage.  
When you take the Attack action on your turn and attack with this weapon, you can make one extra attack as part of that action. That extra attack must be made with the same weapon.  
Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with this weapon, you can make a melee attack roll with the weapon against a second creature within 5 feet of the first that is also within your reach.  
***Whirlwind Attack.*** As an Action, you can make a separate melee attack roll with this weapon against each creature within your reach.
