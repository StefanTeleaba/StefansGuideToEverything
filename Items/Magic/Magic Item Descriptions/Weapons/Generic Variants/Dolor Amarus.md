*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Dolor Amarus
*Weapon (Dagger or Rapier), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon and its Critical Range increases by 1. In addition, the weapon ignores Resistance to Piercing damage. When you score a Critical Hit with this weapon, the target takes 30 additional damage of the weapon's type.
