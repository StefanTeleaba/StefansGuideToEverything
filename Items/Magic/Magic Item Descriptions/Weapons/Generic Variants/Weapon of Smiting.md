*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Mace of Smiting
*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Uncommon*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls when you use this weapon to attack a Construct.  
When you roll a 20 on an attack roll made with this weapon, the target takes an extra 7 Bludgeoning damage, or 14 Bludgeoning damage if it's a Construct. If a Construct has 25 Hit Points or fewer after taking this damage, it is destroyed.

*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The bonus increases to +3 when you use the weapon to attack a Construct.  
When you roll a 20 on an attack roll made with this weapon, the target takes an extra 7 Bludgeoning damage, or 14 Bludgeoning damage if it's a Construct. If a Construct has 25 Hit Points or fewer after taking this damage, it is destroyed.

*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Very Rare*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The bonus increases to +4 when you use the weapon to attack a Construct.  
When you roll a 20 on an attack roll made with this weapon, the target takes an extra 7 Bludgeoning damage, or 14 Bludgeoning damage if it's a Construct. If a Construct has 25 Hit Points or fewer after taking this damage, it is destroyed.
