*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Sanctified Weapon
*Weapon (Any Melee Weapon), Uncommon (Requires Attunement by a Cleric or Paladin)*
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The number of spells you can prepare increases by 1.

*Weapon (Any Melee Weapon), Rare (Requires Attunement by a Cleric or Paladin)*
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. It deals an extra 1d8 Radiant damage to any creature it hits. The number of spells you can prepare increases by 2.  
While you are attuned to this weapon, you can use your Channel Divinity without expending one of the feature's uses. You can't use this property again until the next dawn.

*Weapon (Any Melee Weapon), Very Rare (Requires Attunement by a Cleric or Paladin)*
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. It deals an extra 1d8 Radiant damage to any creature it hits. The number of spells you can prepare increases by 3.  
While you are attuned to this weapon, you gain a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls and to the saving throw DCs of your spells. In addition, you can use your Channel Divinity without expending one of the feature's uses. You can't use this property again until the next dawn.

*Weapon (Any Melee Weapon), Legendary (Requires Attunement by a Cleric or Paladin)*
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. It deals an extra 1d8 Radiant damage to any creature it hits. The number of spells you can prepare increases by 3.  
While you are attuned to this weapon, you gain a +2 bonus to spell attack rolls and to the saving throw DCs of your spells. In addition, you can use your Channel Divinity without expending one of the feature's uses. You can't use this property again until the next dawn.

*Weapon (Any Melee Weapon), Artifact (Requires Attunement by a Cleric or Paladin)*
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. It deals an extra 2d8 Radiant damage to any creature it hits. The number of spells you can prepare increases by 5.  
While you are attuned to this weapon, you gain a +3 bonus to spell attack rolls and to the saving throw DCs of your spells. In addition, you can use your Channel Divinity without expending one of the feature's uses. You can't use this property again until the next dawn.
