*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Thundering Hammer
*Weapon (Light Hammer, Maul, or Warhammer), Uncommon*  
This magic weapon deals an extra 1d8 Thunder damage to any creature it hits. When you score a Critical Hit, the target takes an extra 1d8 Thunder damage and must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or have the Deafened conditions until the end of your next turn.

*Weapon (Light Hammer, Maul, or Warhammer), Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. It deals an extra 1d8 Thunder damage to any creature it hits. When you score a Critical Hit, the target takes an extra 2d8 Thunder damage and must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or have the Blinded and Deafened conditions until the end of your next turn.

*Weapon (Light Hammer, Maul, or Warhammer), Very Rare*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. It deals an extra 1d8 Thunder damage to any creature it hits. When you score a Critical Hit, the target takes an extra 3d8 Thunder damage and must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or have the Blinded and Deafened conditions until the end of your next turn.
