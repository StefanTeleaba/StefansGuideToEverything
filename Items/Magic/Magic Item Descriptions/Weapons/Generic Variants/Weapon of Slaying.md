# Weapon of Slaying
*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Uncommon*  
This magic weapon is meant to slay creatures of a particular type, which the DM chooses or determines randomly by rolling on the table below. If a creature of that type takes damage from the weapon, the creature takes an extra 3d6 damage of the weapon's type.

*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Rare*  
This magic weapon is meant to slay creatures of a particular type, which the DM chooses or determines randomly by rolling on the table below.  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. If a creature of that type takes damage from the weapon, the creature takes an extra 3d6 damage of the weapon's type.

*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Rare*  
This magic weapon is meant to slay creatures of a particular type, which the DM chooses or determines randomly by rolling on the table below.  
If a creature of that type takes damage from the weapon, the creature takes an extra 2d6 damage of the weapon's type. If the target has 25 Hit Points or fewer after taking this damage, it must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be destroyed. On a successful save, the creature has the Frightened condition until the end of your next turn.

*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Rare*  
This magic weapon is meant to slay creatures of a particular type, which the DM chooses or determines randomly by rolling on the table below.  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The bonus increases to +3 when you use the weapon to attack a creature of that type. If you score a Critical Hit against a creature of that type, the target takes an extra 2d6 damage of the weapon's type. If the target has 25 Hit Points or fewer after taking this damage, it is destroyed.

*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Legendary*  
This magic weapon is meant to slay creatures of a particular type, which the DM chooses or determines randomly by rolling on the table below.

# Ammunition of Slaying
*Weapon (Any Ammunition), Very Rare*  
This magic ammunition is meant to slay creatures of a particular type, which the DM chooses or determines randomly by rolling on the table below. If a creature of that type takes damage from the ammunition, the creature makes a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, taking an extra 6d10 Force damage on a failed save or half as much extra damage on a successful one.  
After dealing its extra damage to a creature, the ammunition becomes nonmagical.
