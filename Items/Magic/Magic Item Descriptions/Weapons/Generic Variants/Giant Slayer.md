*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Giant Slayer
*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Uncommon*  
When you hit a Giant with this weapon, the Giant takes an extra 2d6 damage of the weapon’s type and must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or have the Prone condition.

*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.  
When you hit a Giant with this weapon, the Giant takes an extra 2d6 damage of the weapon’s type and must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or have the Prone condition.

*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Very Rare*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.  
When you hit a Giant with this weapon, the Giant takes an extra 2d6 damage of the weapon’s type and must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or have the Prone condition.
