*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Weapon of Disruption
*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Uncommon*  
When you hit a Fiend or an Undead with this magic weapon, that creature takes an extra 1d6 Radiant damage. If the target has 25 Hit Points or fewer after taking this damage, it must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be destroyed. On a successful save, the creature has the Frightened condition until the end of your next turn.  
***Light.*** While you hold this weapon, you can cause it to shed Bright Light in a 20-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 20 feet, either as a Bonus Action or after you roll Initiative, or you can extinguish the light as a Bonus Action.

*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Rare*  
When you hit a Fiend or an Undead with this magic weapon, that creature takes an extra 2d6 Radiant damage. If the target has 25 Hit Points or fewer after taking this damage, it must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be destroyed. On a successful save, the creature has the Frightened condition until the end of your next turn.  
***Light.*** While you hold this weapon, you can cause it to shed Bright Light in a 20-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 20 feet, either as a Bonus Action or after you roll Initiative, or you can extinguish the light as a Bonus Action.

*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Very Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.  
When you hit a Fiend or an Undead with this magic weapon, that creature takes an extra 2d6 Radiant damage. If the target has 25 Hit Points or fewer after taking this damage, it must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or be destroyed. On a successful save, the creature has the Frightened condition until the end of your next turn.  
***Light.*** While you hold this weapon, you can cause it to shed Bright Light in a 30-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 30 feet, either as a Bonus Action or after you roll Initiative, or you can extinguish the light as a Bonus Action.
