*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Eye of the Storm
*Weapon (Falchion or Rapier), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. While you are attuned to this magic weapon, you have Immunity to Lightning and Thunder damage. When you hit with an attack roll using this magic weapon, the target takes an extra 1d8 Lightning damage. When you score a Critical Hit, the target takes an extra 3d8 Thunder damage and you can push it 15 feet away from you.  
***Lightning Leap.*** Once on each of your turns, you can leap horizontally up to 30 feet by spending 10 feet of movement. You turn into a bolt of lightning forming a 30-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line that blasts out from you in a direction you choose. Each creature in the Line takes 1d8 Lightning damage and you reappear at the end of the line.
