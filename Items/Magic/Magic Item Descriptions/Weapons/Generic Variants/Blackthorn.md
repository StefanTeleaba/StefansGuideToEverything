*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Blackthorn
*Weapon (Club or Quarterstaff), Uncommon*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.  
***Forest Harmony.*** When you cast *Shillelagh* on this weapon, it deals an additional 1d8 damage on a Hit.

*Weapon (Club or Quarterstaff), Rare*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.  
***Forest Harmony.*** When you cast *Shillelagh* on this weapon, it deals an additional 1d8 damage on a Hit.  
***Ensnaring Strike.*** When you score a Critical Hit, the target must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or have the Restrained condition for 1 minute. The target or a creature within reach of it can take an action to make a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check, removing the brambles and ending the Restrained condition on the target on a successful check.

*Weapon (Club or Quarterstaff), Very Rare*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.  
***Forest Harmony.*** When you cast *Shillelagh* on this weapon, it deals an additional 1d8 damage on a Hit.  
***Ensnaring Strike.*** When you score a Critical Hit, the target must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or have the Restrained condition for 1 minute. The target or a creature within reach of it can take an action to make a DC 17 Strength (Athletics) check, removing the brambles and ending the Restrained condition on the target on a successful check. While Restrained, the target takes 1d8 Piercing damage at the start of each of its turns.

*Weapon (Club or Quarterstaff), Legendary*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.  
***Forest Harmony.*** When you cast *Shillelagh* on this weapon, it deals an additional 1d8 damage on a Hit.  
***Ensnaring Strike.*** When you score a Critical Hit, the target takes an extra 1d8 Slashing damage and must succeed on a DC 19 Strength saving throw or have the Restrained condition for 1 minute. The target or a creature within reach of it can take an action to make a DC 19 Strength (Athletics) check, removing the brambles and ending the Restrained condition on the target on a successful check. While Restrained, the target takes 2d8 Piercing damage at the start of each of its turns.  
You can use an action to cast one of the following spells from this weapon: *Entangle*, *Plant Growth*, *Spike Growth*. The spells use your spellcasting ability and spell save DC. Once the weapon has been used to cast a spell, it can't be used to cast that spell again until the next dawn.
