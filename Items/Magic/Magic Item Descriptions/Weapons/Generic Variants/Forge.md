*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Forge
*Weapon (Light Hammer, Maul, or Warhammer), Rare*
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon and its Critical Range increases by 1. It deals an extra 1d8 Fire and 1d8 Lightning damage to any creature it hits.

*Weapon (Light Hammer, Maul, or Warhammer), Very Rare*
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon and its Critical Range increases by 1. It deals an extra 1d8 Fire and 1d8 Lightning damage to any creature it hits.

*Weapon (Light Hammer, Maul, or Warhammer), Legendary*
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon and its Critical Range increases by 1. It deals an extra 1d8 Fire and 1d8 Lightning damage to any creature it hits. When you score a Critical Hit, the target takes an extra 2d8 Fire damage and 2d8 Lightning damage.
