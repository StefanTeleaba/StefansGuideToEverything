*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Hew
*Weapon (Battleaxe, Greataxe, or Handaxe), Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The weapon deals maximum damage if the target is a Plant or a nonmagical wooden object that isn't being worn or carried.

# Hew
*Weapon (Battleaxe), Uncommon*  
You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.  
Hew is a +1 battleaxe that deals maximum damage when the wielder attacks a Plant creature or an object made of wood. The axe's creator used it for protection in the forest while he cut firewood.
