# Marksman's Bow

# Marksman's Bow
*Weapon (any bow), uncommon (requires attunement by a ranger)*  
Whenever you cast the hunter's mark spell using a class feature or spell slot, you can also make a single weapon attack with this bow against the same target you mark with the spell.  
*"Your hands must keep pace with your instincts." –Virgo, hunter of the eastern woods, to his apprentice.*
