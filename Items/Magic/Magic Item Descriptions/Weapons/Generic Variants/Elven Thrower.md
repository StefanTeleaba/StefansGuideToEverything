# Elven Thrower
*Weapon (Javelin, Spear, or Trident), Very Rare*
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. It has the Thrown property with a normal range of 30 feet and a long range of 120 feet, and it returns to your hand immediately after you use it to make a ranged attack roll. This weapon deals an extra 1d8 Force damage on a hit, or an extra 2d8 Force damage if the target is a Giant.

# Elven Thrower
*Weapon (Spear), Very Rare (Requires Attunement By a Elf)*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. It has the thrown property with a normal range of 20 feet and a long range of 60 feet. When you hit with a ranged attack using this weapon, it deals an extra 1d8 damage or, if the target is a Giant, 2d8 damage. Immediately after the attack, the weapon flies back to your hand.
