*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Keen Weapon
*Weapon (Any Melee Weapon with the Finesse Property), Uncommon*  
The Critical Range of this magic weapon increases by 1 and when you score a Critical Hit with it, the weapon deals one additional die of damage.

*Weapon (Any Melee Weapon with the Finesse Property), Rare*  
The Critical Range of this magic weapon increases by 2 and when you score a Critical Hit with it, the weapon deals two additional dice of damage.

*Weapon (Any Melee Weapon with the Finesse Property), Very Rare*  
The Critical Range of this magic weapon increases by 3 and when you score a Critical Hit with it, the weapon deals three additional dice of damage.

# Keen
This ability doubles the threat range of a weapon. Only piercing or slashing weapons can be keen. (If you roll this property randomly for an inappropriate weapon, reroll.) This benefit doesn’t stack with any other effect that expands the threat range of a weapon (such as the keen edge spell or the Improved Critical feat).  
Moderate transmutation; CL 10th; Craft Magic Arms and Armor, keen edge; Price +1 bonus.
