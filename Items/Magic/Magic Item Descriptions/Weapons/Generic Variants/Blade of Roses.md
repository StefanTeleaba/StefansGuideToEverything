# Blade of Roses
*Weapon (Dagger, Greatsword, Longsword, or Rapier), Uncommon*  
As long as this weapon is within your reach and you are attuned to it, your Charisma score increases by 2, to a maximum of 20.

*Weapon (Dagger, Greatsword, Longsword, or Rapier), Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. In addiiton, as long as it is within your reach and you are attuned to it, your Charisma score increases by 2, to a maximum of 22.

*Weapon (Dagger, Greatsword, Longsword, or Rapier), Very Rare*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. In addiiton, as long as it is within your reach and you are attuned to it, your Charisma score increases by 2, to a maximum of 24.
