*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Echoblade
*Weapon (Longsword), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. Its Critical Range increases by 1 and it has the Finesse Property.  
***Echoing Strikes.*** When you expend one Bardic Inspiration Die, you have Advantage on attack rolls with this weapon until the start of your next turn. If any attack hits during that round, the target takes extra Thunder damage equal to your Charisma modifier plus your Bard level.
