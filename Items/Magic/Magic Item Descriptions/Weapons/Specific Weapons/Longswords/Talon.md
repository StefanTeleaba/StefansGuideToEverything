*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Talon
*Weapon (Longsword), Uncommon*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. In addition, the weapon glows faintly when Orcs are within 120 feet of it.

*Weapon (Longsword), Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The weapon glows faintly when Orcs are within 120 feet of it and the bonus increases to +3 when you use it to attack an Orc. In addition, when you hit an Orc with this magic weapon, the weapon deals two additional dice of damage of the weapon's type.

*Weapon (Longsword), Very Rare*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The weapon glows faintly when Orcs are within 120 feet of it and the bonus increases to +4 when you use it to attack an Orc. In addition, when you hit an Orc with this magic weapon, the weapon deals three additional dice of damage of the weapon's type.

*Weapon (Longsword), Legendary*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The weapon glows faintly when Orcs are within 120 feet of it and the bonus increases to +5 when you use it to attack an Orc. In addition, when you hit an Orc with this magic weapon, the weapon deals three additional dice of damage of the weapon's type.

Sir Aldith died fighting off the bandits that attacked through the hidden caverns below his manor. Talon was lost here until Ssarnak found it.
