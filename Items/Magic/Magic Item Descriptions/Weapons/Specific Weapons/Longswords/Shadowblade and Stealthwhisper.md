*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Shadowblade and Stealthwhisper
Shadowblade and Stealthwhisper are the twin magic longswords of Mask. Attuning to both swords only uses one Attunement Slot.
## Shadowblade
*Weapon (Longsword), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon, it has the Light property, and its Critical Range increases by 1. When you hit with an attack roll using this magic weapon, the target takes an extra 1d8 Cold damage. When you take the Attack action on your turn and attack with this weapon, you can make one extra attack as part of that action. That extra attack must be made with the same weapon.
## Stealthwhisper
*Weapon (Longsword), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon, it has the Light property, and its Critical Range increases by 1. When you hit with an attack roll using this magic weapon, the target takes an extra 1d8 Cold damage. When you take the Attack action on your turn and attack with this weapon, you can make one extra attack as part of that action. That extra attack must be made with the same weapon.
