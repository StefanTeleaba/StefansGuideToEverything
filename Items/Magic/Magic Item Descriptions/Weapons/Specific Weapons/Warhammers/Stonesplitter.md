*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Stonesplitter
*Weapon (Warhammer), Legendary*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon and when you score a Critical Hit with it, the weapon deals three additional dice of damage.  
Whenever this weapon hits an object, the hit is a Critical Hit, you maximize your weapon damage dice against the target, and you can have it deal your choice of Piercing, Force, or Thunder damage instead of Bludgeoning damage.  
In addition, this magic weapon can't be broken. Special means must be used to destroy it.
