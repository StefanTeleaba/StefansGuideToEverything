# Javelin of Fire
*Weapon (Javelin), Uncommon*  
Each time you make an attack roll with this magic weapon and hit, you can have it deal Fire damage instead of Piercing damage.  
***Fire Blast.*** When you throw this weapon at a target you can speak its command word. On a hit, the target takes an extra 1d6 Fire damage. Hit or miss, the shard then explodes. The target and each creature within 20 feet of it makes a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. This property can't be used again until the next dawn.

*Weapon (Javelin), Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. Each time you make an attack roll with this magic weapon and hit, you can have it deal Fire damage instead of Piercing damage. Whether you deal Fire damage or the weapon's normal damage type, the attack deals an extra 1d6 Fire damage.  
***Fire Blast.*** When you throw this weapon at a target you can speak its command word. On a hit, the target takes an extra 1d6 Fire damage. Hit or miss, the shard then explodes. The target and each creature within 20 feet of it makes a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 5d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. This property can't be used again until the next dawn.

*Weapon (Javelin), Very Rare*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. Each time you make an attack roll with this magic weapon and hit, you can have it deal Fire damage instead of Piercing damage. Whether you deal Fire damage or the weapon's normal damage type, the target and each creature of your choice within 5 feet of it takes an extra 1d6 Fire damage.  
***Fire Blast.*** When you throw this weapon at a target you can speak its command word. On a hit, the target takes an extra 1d6 Fire damage. Hit or miss, the shard then explodes. The target and each creature within 20 feet of it makes a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 7d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. This property can't be used again until the next dawn.

# Blast Javelin
*Weapon (javelin), uncommon*  
This javelin radiates heat from within when thrown. When you make a ranged attack with this magic weapon and speak its command word, it becomes primed to detonate. If the target is a creature and the attack hits, the javelin immediately explodes; on a miss, the javelin instead lands at a random point beyond the target within 30 feet of it. If the attack misses, or if the target isn't a creature (such as if you threw it at a point or an object), the javelin instead explodes at its location at the start of your next turn. If a creature moves the javelin before then, it explodes early.  
When the javelin explodes, each creature within 20 feet of it must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw; the original target of the attack makes the saving throw with disadvantage. A creature takes 3d6 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.  
Once the command word has been used, it can't be used again for 1 minute. After that time, it shouldn't be used again until the next dawn: each time it's used again before then, the javelin has a 50 percent chance to destroy itself when it explodes.
