*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Kurwin's Cauteriser
*Weapon (Scimitar), Uncommon*  
This magic weapon deals an extra 1d4 Fire damage to any creature it hits and the target starts Burning.

*Weapon (Scimitar), Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. It deals an extra 1d6 Fire damage to any creature it hits and the target starts Burning.

---

*Baldur's Gate 3*  
*Mortician Kurwin was known to use his trusty Cauteriser on all sorts of interesting subjects. At first there were cadavers, then people who wouldn't be missed, and it was only after he targeted a well-liked tavern owner that he was ratted out to the law.*
