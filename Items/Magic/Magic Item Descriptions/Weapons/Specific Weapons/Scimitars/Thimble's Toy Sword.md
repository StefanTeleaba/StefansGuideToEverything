*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Thimble's Toy Sword
*Weapon (Scimitar), Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit an incorporeal foe with this magic weapon, that creature takes an extra 2d6 damage of the weapon's type.

---

*Critical Role: Campaign Four*  
*This toy longsword was a gift from Ulbid Morn, who made it for his daughter Cerebel.*
