# Scimitar of the Silver Naga
*Weapon (scimitar), very rare (requires attunement)*  
This magic scimitar deals an extra 1d6 poison damage to any creature it hits. While attuned to it, you have resistance to poison damage and have advantage on saving throws against the poisoned condition.  
***Incinerating Serpent.*** While holding the scimitar, you can use an action to speak its command word and create an instantaneous naga of pure light. Choose a space that you can see within 30 feet of you for the naga to begin in: it then instantly extends from that space in a contiguous line, which is composed of up to ten 5-foot-cubes. Each cube can be placed anywhere—vertically or hozontally—directly next to the previous one. Each creature within the line must then make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 8d6 radiant damage and is poisoned for 1 minute; a poisoned creature is also blinded for the duration. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much radiant damage and isn't poisoned. A creature poisoned in this way can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. This property can't be used again until the next dawn.  
***Naga's Restoration.*** If you die while holding the scimitar, it loses this and its Incinerating Serpent properties. You are then restored to half your maximum number of hit points and are cured of any conditions affecting you.  
*The silver glint of pure radiance: when you stare into its purity, do you feel its cruel sting?*
