*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Sword of Shadows
*Weapon (Greatsword), Legendary*  
You gain a +5 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon and its Critical Range increases by 1. When you hit with an attack roll using this magic weapon, the target takes an extra 2d6 Cold damage. When you score a Critical hit with this magoc weapon, the target and each creature of your choice within 5 feet of it takes 4d6 Cold damage.

---

*Faiths and Pantheons*  
*The Sword of Shadows, favored weapon of the Faerûnian traveler god Shaundakul, was a +5 anarchic icy burst keen greatsword.*
