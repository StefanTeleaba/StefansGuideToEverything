# Hook
*Weapon (Rapier), Uncommon*  
This magic weapon has the Light property. If another creature hits you with a melee attack, you can take a Reaction to add your Dexterity modifier to your Armor Class, potentially causing the attack to miss you. You gain this bonus to your AC against melee attacks until the start of your next turn.

*Weapon (Rapier), Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. It has the Light property. If another creature hits you with a melee attack, you can take a Reaction to add your Dexterity modifier to your Armor Class, potentially causing the attack to miss you. You gain this bonus to your AC against melee attacks until the start of your next turn.

*Weapon (Rapier), Very Rare*

If another creature hits you with a melee attack, you can take a Reaction to add your Dexterity modifier to your Armor Class, potentially causing the attack to miss you. You gain this bonus to your AC against melee attacks until the start of your next turn.
