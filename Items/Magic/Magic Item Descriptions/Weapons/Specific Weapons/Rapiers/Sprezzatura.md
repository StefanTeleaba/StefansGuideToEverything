*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Sprezzatura
*Weapon (Rapier), Legendary*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.  
***Parry, Riposte and Disarm.*** While you hold this weapon, when another creature hits you with a melee attack, you can take a Reaction to add 5 to your AC against that attack and make one attack with it against that creature. If the attack hits, you can either deal damage or cause the target to drop one object of your choice that it's holding, with the object landing in its space.
