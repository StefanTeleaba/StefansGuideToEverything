# Devotee's Censer
*Weapon (Flail), Uncommon (Requires Attunement by a Cleric or Paladin)*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon and can use it as a Holy Symbol.  
While holding this magic weapon, you can take a Bonus Action and use a command word to cause a thin cloud of incense to emanate in a 30-foot Emanation originating from you. When you create the aura and at the start of each of your turns while it persists, you and your allies in the Emanation each regain 1d4 Hit Points. The incense lasts for 1 minute or until you take a Bonus Action to issue the command again or until you drop or stow the weapon. Once you use this property, it can’t be used again until the next dawn.

*Tasha's Cauldron of Everything 5e p.126*
# Devotee's Censer
*Weapon (flail), rare (requires attunement by a cleric or paladin)*  
The rounded head of this flail is perforated with tiny holes, arranged in symbols and patterns. The flail counts as a holy symbol for you. When you hit with an attack using this magic flail, the target takes an extra 1d8 radiant damage.  
As a bonus action, you can speak the command word to cause the flail to emanate a thin cloud of incense out to 10 feet for 1 minute. At the start of each of your turns, you and any other creatures in the incense each regain 1d4 hit points. This property can't be used again until the next dawn.
