*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Bastard's Inheritance
*Weapon (Shortsword), Uncommon*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit with an attack roll using this magic weapon, the target takes an extra 1d6 Poison damage.  

*Weapon (Shortsword), Rare*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit with an attack roll using this magic weapon, the target takes an extra 1d6 Poison damage and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of your next turn.

*Weapon (Shortsword), Very Rare*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit with an attack roll using this magic weapon, the target takes an extra 2d6 Poison damage and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of your next turn.
