# Carrion War Pick
*Weapon (War Pick), Uncommon*  
When you hit a creature that is missing any of its Hit Points with this weapon, the target takes one additional die of damage of the weapon's type and you can have the weapon deal Necrotic damage instead of Piercing damage. In addition, when you reduce an enemy to 0 Hit Points with this weapon, you gain Temporary Hit Points equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.

*Weapon (War Pick), Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit a creature that is missing any of its Hit Points with this weapon, the target takes one additional die of damage of the weapon's type and you can have the weapon deal Necrotic damage instead of Piercing damage. In addition, when you reduce an enemy to 0 Hit Points with this weapon, you gain Temporary Hit Points equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.

# Carrion War Pick
*Weapon (war pick), uncommon*  
If you hit a creature that is missing any of its hit points using this magic weapon, its damage die becomes a d10 for the attack, and it deals necrotic damage instead of piercing. Furthermore, when you slay a Small or larger living creature with this weapon, you immediately gain 1d10 temporary hit points; you lose these hit points early if you're no longer holding the weapon.
