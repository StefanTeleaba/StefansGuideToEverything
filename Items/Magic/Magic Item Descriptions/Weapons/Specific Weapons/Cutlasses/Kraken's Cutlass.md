# Kraken's Cutlass
*Weapon (Cutlass or Scimitar), Uncommon*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. It has the Thrown property with a normal range of 15 feet and no long range. It returns to your hand immediately after you use it to make a ranged attack roll. If you score a Critical Hit, the target must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or have the Blinded condition for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.

*Weapon (Cutlass or Scimitar), Rare*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. It has the Thrown property with a normal range of 20 feet and no long range. It returns to your hand immediately after you use it to make a ranged attack roll. If you score a Critical Hit, the target must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or have the Blinded and Poisoned conditions for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.

*Weapon (Cutlass or Scimitar), Very Rare*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. It has the Thrown property with a normal range of 30 feet and no long range. It returns to your hand immediately after you use it to make a ranged attack roll. If you score a Critical Hit, the target must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or have the Blinded and Poisoned conditions for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success. In addition, immediately after you score a Critical Hit, you can move up to half your Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

*Weapon (Cutlass or Scimitar), Legendary*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. It has the Thrown property with a normal range of 30 feet and no long range. It returns to your hand immediately after you use it to make a ranged attack roll. If you score a Critical Hit, the target must succeed on a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or have the Blinded and Poisoned conditions for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the conditions on itself on a success. In addition, immediately after you score a Critical Hit, you can move up to half your Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.  
You can use an action to cast one of the following spells from the Kraken's Cutlass: *Call Lightning*, *Control Weather*, *Darkness*. The spells use your spellcasting ability and spell save DC. Once the weapon has been used to cast a spell, it can't be used to cast that spell again until the next dawn.

# Kraken's Cutlass
*Weapon (scimitar), rare*  
This dark scimitar has inky swirls throughout its blade. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. Once per turn when you would make a melee attack with it, you can choose to throw it at a creature that you can see within 15 feet of you instead (as if it had the thrown property). When you do, the blade's tentacle-like guard extends, but it remains gripped to your outstretched hand; hit or miss, the blade then retracts to your hand after the attack.  
In addition, when you roll a 20 on an attack roll made with this weapon, the target must also succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature is covered by a spray of magical squid ink and is blinded for 1 minute. The creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns for the duration, ending the effect early on a success.  
*Many are those who seek the salvation of the shore. What those poor unfortunate souls fail to realize: you are never truly beyond the kraken's reach.*
