*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Impaler
*Weapon (Fullblade), Artifact (Requires Attunement)*

You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

***Morph Weapon.*** You can transform this weapon into any Simple or Martial Weapon of your choice. When you do, you can choose the weapon's Properties and Mastery Property. Once you use this property, it can’t be used again until the next dawn.

***Weapon Bond.*** While you are attuned to this magic weapon, you can't be disarmed unless you have the Incapacitated condition. If it is on the same plane of existence, you can summon it as a Bonus Action, causing it to teleport instantly to your hand.

***Evil Dragon Vanquisher.*** When you hit a Dragon of Evil Alignment with this magic weapon, that creature has Vulnerability to the damage.
