# Infernal Enforcer
*Weapon (pistol), legendary (requires attunement)*  
This pistol has a mote of hellfire at the center of its 6-shot cylinder; it magically melts any nonmagical firearm bullet that's loaded in it, destroying the bullet. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon, and it deals an extra 1d6 fire damage to any target it hits.  
The pistol has 6 charges for the following properties. It regains 1 expended charge at the start of each of your turns and when you roll a 6 on any fire damage die you roll using the weapon. You can also use an action while holding the pistol to cause it to regain all of its expended charges; you do so by flipping out and spinning the weapon's cylinder, which emits a magma-like glow from within.  
The weapon doesn't use ammunition and therefore lacks the loading property. Instead, each time you make a ranged attack with it, the weapon must expend 1 of its charges to magically create and fire a bullet made of brimstone.  
***Bedeviling Curse.*** When you hit a creature with a ranged attack using the pistol, you can expend 1 additional charge as part of the attack to force that creature to make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature is cursed by a plume of devilish smoke that magically weakens it. While cursed in this way, the creature takes a cumulative −1 penalty to any attack roll it makes (to a maximum penalty of −3), and it has disadvantage on any saving throw it makes against this weapon's named properties. This curse lasts for 1 minute, until you're incapacitated, or until you curse another creature with it.  
***Fan the Hammer and Flames.*** Once per turn when you take the Attack action with the pistol, you can forgo one of your attacks with it to expend 3 of the pistol's charges and release a 15-foot cone of damning flames from the holes in the barrel and cylinder. Each creature within the cone must succeed on a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw or take 1d10 piercing damage and 4d6 fire damage.
