*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Candle Mace
*Weapon (Mace), Common*  
You can take an action to cause the item to shed Bright Light in a 5-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 5 feet, or to extinguish the light.

*Weapon (Mace), Uncommon*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. You can take an action to cause the item to shed Bright Light in a 5-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 5 feet, or to extinguish the light.

# Candle Mace
*Weapon (Mace), Uncommon*  
You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The head of this mace sheds bright light in a 5-foot-radius and dim light for an additional 5 feet. When you wield this mace, you can extinguish or ignite its light as an action.
