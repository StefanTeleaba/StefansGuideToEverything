*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Ethanniel's Sling
*Weapon (Sling), Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. While you are attuned to it, your Dexterity increases by 2, to a maximum of 20.  
***Magic Missile.*** If you do not nock a bullet, a magical bullet of magical force appears nocked and ready to fire. A bullet produced by this weapon strikes a creature of your choice that you can see within 120 feet. A bullet deals 1d4+1 Force damage to its target.

*Weapon (Sling), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. While you are attuned to it, your Dexterity increases by 2, to a maximum of 22.  
***Magic Missile.*** If you do not nock a bullet, a magical bullet of magical force appears nocked and ready to fire. A bullet produced by this weapon strikes a creature of your choice that you can see within 120 feet. A bullet deals 2d4+2 Force damage to its target.

---

*Pool of Radiance: Ruins of Myth Drannor*  
*Ethanniel's sling was designed like many other ancient elven slings, a hand-held solid curved launching device that propelled round bullets toward its targets with a single swing, unlike regular leather slings.*
