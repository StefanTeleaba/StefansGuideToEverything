# Keen Sdortbow
*Weapon (Shortbow), Uncommon*  
The Critical Range of this magic weapon increases by 1 and when you score a Critical Hit with it, the weapon deals one additional die of damage.

*Weapon (Shortbow), Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon, its Critical Range increases by 1 and when you score a Critical Hit with it, the weapon deals one additional die of damage.

*Weapon (Shortbow), Very Rare+*  
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon, its Critical Range increases by 2 and when you score a Critical Hit with it, the weapon deals two additional dice of damage.

*Weapon (Shortbow), Legendary+*  
You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon, its Critical Range increases by 3 and when you score a Critical Hit with it, the weapon deals three additional dice of damage.
