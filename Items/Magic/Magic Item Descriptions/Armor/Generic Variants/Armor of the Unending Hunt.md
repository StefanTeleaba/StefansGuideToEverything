*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Armor of the Unending Hunt
*Armor (Chain Mail or Chain Shirt), Uncommon*  
You are considered trained with this armor even if you lack training with Medium or Heavy Armor, and you have Advantage on saving throws to avoid Exhaustion. If the armor normally imposes Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks or has a Strength requirement, this version of the armor doesn’t.

*Armor (Chain Mail or Chain Shirt), Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class while you wear this armor. You are considered trained with this armor even if you lack training with Medium or Heavy Armor, and you have Immunity to the Exhausted condition. If the armor normally imposes Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks or has a Strength requirement, this version of the armor doesn’t.

*Armor (Chain Mail or Chain Shirt), Very Rare*  
You gain a +2 bonus to Armor Class while you wear this armor. You are considered trained with this armor even if you lack training with Medium or Heavy Armor, and you have Immunity to the Exhausted condition. If the armor normally imposes Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks or has a Strength requirement, this version of the armor doesn’t.

# Armor of the Unending Hunt
This mithral +2 chainmail armor was built by the elves for rangers on long-range patrols. In addition to its protective qualities, it provides the wearer with immunity to fatigue and exhaustion.  
Moderate Abjuration; CL 8th; Craft Magic Arms and Armor, restoration; Price 21,500 gp; Cost 10,900 gp + 848 XP; Weight 20 lb.
