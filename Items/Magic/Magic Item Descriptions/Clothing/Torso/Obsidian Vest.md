*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Obsidian Vest
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

If you take Fire damage while wearing this robe, the robe reduces the damage you take by 10.

---

*The inside of this vest is dark and faceted like volcanic glass, yet the material is light and soft. Against the skin, it also produces a heat like a toasty fire on a day of snow.*
