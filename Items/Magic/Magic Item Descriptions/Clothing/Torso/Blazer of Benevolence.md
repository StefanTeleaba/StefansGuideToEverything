*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Blazer of Benevolence
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*  
When you inspire another creature using your Bardic Inspiration, you gain a number of Temporary Hit Points equal to your Charisma modifier.

*Wondrous Item, Rare*  
When you inspire another creature using your Bardic Inspiration, you gain a number of Temporary Hit Points equal to your Charisma modifier plus your Bard level.

*Even though the embroidered velvet won't help against a sharp blade, wearing it fills you with self-assuredness.*
