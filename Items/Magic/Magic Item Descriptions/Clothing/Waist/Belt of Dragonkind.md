*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Belt of Dragonkind
*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
While wearing this belt, you gain the following benefits:  
***Draconic.*** You know Draconic.  
***Friend of Dragonkind.*** You have Advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks made to interact with dragons and dragonborn.  
***Toughness.*** Your Constitution increases by 2, to a maximum of 20. In addition, while attuned to the belt, you have a 50 percent chance each day at dawn of being covered by dragon-like scales.  
If you aren't a dragon or dragonborn, you gain the following additional benefits while wearing the belt:  
***Darkvision.*** You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.  
***Resilience.*** You have Resistance to Poison damage. You also have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.
