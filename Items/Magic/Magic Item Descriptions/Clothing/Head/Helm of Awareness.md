*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Helm of Awareness
*Wondrous Item, Common*  
While wearing this helmet, you have Advantage on Initiative rolls.

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*  
While wearing this helmet, you have Advantage on Initiative rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks.

*Wondrous Item, Rare*  
While wearing this helmet, you gain the following benefits:  
***All-Around Vision.*** You have Advantage on Initiative rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks.  
***Special Senses.*** You have Darkvision with a range of 120 feet.

*Wondrous Item, Very Rare*  
While wearing this helmet, you gain the following benefits:  
***All-Around Vision.*** You gain a +1 bonus to AC and you have Advantage on Initiative rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks.  
***Special Senses.*** You have Blindsight and Darkvision, both with a range of 120 feet.

*Wondrous Item, Legendary*  
While wearing this helmet, you gain the following benefits:  
***All-Around Vision.*** You gain a +1 bonus to AC and you have Advantage on Initiative rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks.  
***Special Senses.*** You have Blindsight, Darkvision and Truesight, all with a range of 120 feet.
