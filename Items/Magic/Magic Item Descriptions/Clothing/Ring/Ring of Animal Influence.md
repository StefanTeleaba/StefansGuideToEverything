*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Ring of Animal Influence
*Ring, Rare*  
While wearing the ring, you can cast the following spells (save DC 15) from it: *Animal Friendship*, *Fear* (affects Beasts only), *Speak with Animals*. Once the ring has been used to cast a spell, it can't be used to cast that spell again until the next dawn.

*Ring, Very Rare*  
While wearing the ring, you can cast the following spells (save DC 17) from it without expending a spell slot: *Animal Friendship*, *Animal Messenger*, *Beast Sense*, *Dominate Beast*, *Fear* (affects Beasts only), *Speak with Animals*.

# Ring of Animal Influence
*Ring, Rare*  
This ring has 3 charges, and it regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the ring, you can expend 1 charge to cast one of the following spells (save DC 13) from it:
- Animal Friendship
- Fear (affects Beasts only)
- Speak with Animals

# Ring of Animal Friendship
On command, this ring affects an animal as if the wearer had cast charm animal.  
Faint enchantment; CL 3rd; Forge Ring, charm animal; Price 10,800 gp.
