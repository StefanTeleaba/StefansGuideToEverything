# Covolt Ring
*Ring, Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
When you take Lightning damage while wearing this magic ring, you can take a Reaction to halve the Lightning damage you take. When you do, creatures of your choice in a 10-foot Emanation originating from you make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 15), taking Lightning damage equal to the amount of damage you reduced on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.  
***Electric Field.*** As a Bonus Action, you can create an electrical field that surrounds your body for 1 minute or until you dismiss it (no action required). Whenever a creature ends its turn within 10 feet of you or hits you with a melee attack roll while the field is active, it takes 1d8 Lightning damage. Once this property is used, it can’t be used again until the next dawn.

*Ring, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
When you take Lightning damage while wearing this magic ring, you can take a Reaction to halve the Lightning damage you take. When you do, creatures of your choice in a 10-foot Emanation originating from you make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 15), taking Lightning damage equal to the amount of damage you reduced on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.  
***Electric Field.*** As a Bonus Action, you can create an electrical field that surrounds your body for 1 minute or until you dismiss it (no action required). Whenever a creature ends its turn within 10 feet of you or hits you with a melee attack roll while the field is active, it takes 2d8 Lightning damage. Once this property is used, it can’t be used again until the next dawn.  
***Empower Lightning Spell.*** When you use a spell slot to cast a spell that deals Lightning damage and can be cast with a higher-level spell slot, you can increase the spell's effective level by 1. When you cast a cantrip that deals Lightning damage, that cantrip deals an additonal die of damage.

# Covolt Ring
*Ring, rare (requires attunement)*  
This ring is made with covolt, an elemental blue metal that carries stormlike properties. If you take lightning damage while wearing the ring, you can use your reaction to halve that damage. When you do, each creature within 5 feet of you (excluding yourself) must immediately succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take lightning damage equal to the amount you reduced.  
While wearing the ring, you can use an action to create an electrical field around you. The field remains for 1 minute, until you end the effect (no action required), or until you're no longer wearing the ring. A creature takes 1d8 lightning damage from the field if it ends its turn within 5 feet of you or if it hits you with a melee attack while within that range. Once you create the field, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.
