*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Ring of Vitality
*Ring, Uncommon*  
While wearing this ring, your Hit Point maximum increases by 1 for each level you have attained.

*Ring, Rare*  
While wearing this ring, your Hit Point maximum increases by 2 for each level you have attained.

*Ring, Very Rare*  
While wearing this ring, your Hit Point maximum increases by 3 for each level you have attained.

*Ring, Legendary*  
While wearing this ring, your Hit Point maximum increases by 5 for each level you have attained.

*Ring, Legendary++ (Requires Attunement)*  
While wearing this ring, your Hit Point maximum increases by 10 for each level you have attained.
