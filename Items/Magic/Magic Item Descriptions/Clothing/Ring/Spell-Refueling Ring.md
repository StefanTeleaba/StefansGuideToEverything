*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Spell-Refueling Ring
*Ring, Uncommon (Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)*  
This ring has 3 charges and recovers all expended charges daily at dawn. As a Bonus Action, you can recover a spell slot of a level equal to the number of expended charges.

*Ring, Rare (Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)*  
This ring has 5 charges and recovers all expended charges daily at dawn. As a Bonus Action, you can recover a spell slot of a level equal to the number of expended charges.

*Ring, Very Rare (Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)*  
This ring has 7 charges and recovers all expended charges daily at dawn. As a Bonus Action, you can recover a spell slot of a level equal to the number of expended charges. You can not recover a spell slot higher than level 5.

*Ring, Legendary (Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)*  
This ring has 10 charges and recovers all expended charges daily at dawn. As a Bonus Action, you can recover a spell slot of a level equal to the number of expended charges. You can not recover a spell slot higher than level 5.

# Spell-Refueling Ring
*Ring, Uncommon (Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)*  
While wearing this ring, you can recover one expended spell slot as a Bonus Action. The recovered slot can be of level 3 or lower. Once used, the ring can’t be used again until the next dawn.
