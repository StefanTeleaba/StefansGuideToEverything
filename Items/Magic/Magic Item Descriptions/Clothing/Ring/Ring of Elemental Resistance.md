# Ring of Elemental Resistance
*Ring, Legendary (Requires Attunement)*  
You have Resistance to Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning and Thunder damage while wearing this ring. This ring is set with Pearl, Tourmaline, Garnet, Citrine and Spinel.

*Ring, Legendary+*  
You have Resistance to Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning and Thunder damage while wearing this ring. This ring is set with Pearl, Tourmaline, Garnet, Citrine and Spinel.
