*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Ring of Warmth
*Ring, Uncommon*  
If you take Cold damage while wearing this ring, the ring reduces the damage you take by 10. In addition, while wearing this ring, you and everything you wear and carry are unharmed by temperatures of 0 degrees Fahrenheit or lower.

*Ring, Rare*  
If you take Cold damage while wearing this ring, the ring reduces the damage you take by 20. In addition, while wearing this ring, you and everything you wear and carry are unharmed by temperatures of 0 degrees Fahrenheit or lower.

*Ring, Very Rare*  
If you take Cold damage while wearing this ring, the ring reduces the damage you take by 30. In addition, while wearing this ring, you and everything you wear and carry are unharmed by temperatures of 0 degrees Fahrenheit or lower.

*Ring, Legendary*  
If you take Cold damage while wearing this ring, the ring reduces the damage you take by 50. In addition, while wearing this ring, you and everything you wear and carry are unharmed by temperatures of 0 degrees Fahrenheit or lower.

# Ring of Warmth
*Ring, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*
