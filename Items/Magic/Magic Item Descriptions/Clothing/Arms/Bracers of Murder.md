*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Bracers of Murder
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*  
While wearing these bracers, you gain a +1 bonus to the saving throw DCs of your rogue features. In addition, whenever you roll for your Sneak Attack damage, you can reroll any 1 on the dice.

*Wondrous Item, Rare*  
While wearing these bracers, you gain a +2 bonus to the saving throw DCs of your rogue features and whenever you roll for your Sneak Attack damage, you can reroll any 1 or 2 on the dice. In addition, during the first round of each combat, you have a +1 bonus to attack rolls against any creature that hasn't taken a turn. If your Sneak Attack hits any target during that round, the target takes an extra 1d6 damage of the weapon's type.

*Wondrous Item, Very Rare*  
While wearing these bracers, you gain a +3 bonus to the saving throw DCs of your rogue features and whenever you roll for your Sneak Attack damage, you can reroll any 1 or 2 on the dice. In addition, during the first round of each combat, you have a +2 bonus to attack rolls against any creature that hasn't taken a turn. If your Sneak Attack hits any target during that round, the target takes an extra 2d6 damage of the weapon's type.

# Bracers of Murder
**Price (Item Level):** 8,000 gp (11th)  
**Body Slot:** Arms  
**Caster Level:** 5th  
**Aura:** Faint; (DC 17) evocation  
**Activation:** —  
**Weight:** 3 lb.  
*Each of these burnished metal bracers is adorned with grinning skull set with onyx stones in its eye sockets.*  
Bracers of murder grant you a +2 profane bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls against flat-footed targets.  
If you have the death attack ability, the save DC to resist your death attack increases by 2.  
If you have sneak attack or sudden strike, reroll any result of 1 on the dice.  
*Prerequisite:* Craft Wondrous Item, divine favor, evil.  
*Cost to Create:* 4,000 gp, 200 XP, 8 days.

---

**Flat-Footed.** A character who has not yet acted during a combat is flat-footed, not yet reacting normally to the situation. A flat-footed character loses his Dexterity bonus to AC (if any) and cannot make attacks of opportunity.
