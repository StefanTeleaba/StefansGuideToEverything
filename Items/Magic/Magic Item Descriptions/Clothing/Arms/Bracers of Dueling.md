*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Bracers of Dueling
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*  
While wearing these bracers, you have proficiency with all Martial Melee weapons that have the Finesse or Light property, and you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls made with such weapons.

*Wondrous Item, Rare*  
While wearing these bracers, you have proficiency with all Martial Melee weapons that have the Finesse or Light property, and you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and a +2 bonus to damage rolls made with such weapons.

*Wondrous Item, Very Rare*  
While wearing these bracers, you have proficiency with all Martial Melee weapons that have the Finesse or Light property, and you gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and a +4 bonus to damage rolls made with such weapons.

---

***Blade Master.*** When you attack with a Greatsword, a Longsword, a Rapier, a Scimitar or a Shortsword, you can activate the Graze, Sap, or Vex property in addition to a different mastery property you’re using with that weapon.  
***Opportunist.***  While you're holding a Greatsword, a Longsword, a Rapier, a Scimitar or a Shortsword, you have Advantage on Opportunity Attacks.  
***Parrying Stance.*** While you're holding a Greatsword, a Longsword, a Rapier, a Scimitar or a Shortsword, you can take an Action to add your Proficiency Bonus to your Armor Class until the start of your next turn.
