# Sanguine Vambraces
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*  
When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can replace one of the attacks to expend two Hit Dice and cast *Vampiric Touch* from the braces. The spell uses your spellcasting ability and spell save DC. Until the spell ends, when you take the Attack action on your turn, you can replace one of the attacks with a spell attack from the spell.

*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can replace one of the attacks to expend three Hit Dice and cast *Vampiric Touch (Level 4, Duration: 1 minute)* from the braces. The spell uses your spellcasting ability and spell save DC. Until the spell ends, when you take the Attack action on your turn, you can replace one of the attacks with a spell attack from the spell.

*Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
Immediately after hittiing a creature with a Melee weapon or an Unarmed Strike, you can take a Bonus Action to expend one Hit Die and siphon life from the target. The target takes an extra 3d6 Necrotic damage, and you regain Hit Points equal to half the amount of Necrotic damage dealt.  
Alternatively, when you take the Attack action on your turn, you can replace one of the attacks to expend three Hit Dice and cast *Vampiric Touch (Level 5, Duration: 1 minute)* from the braces. The spell uses your spellcasting ability and spell save DC. Until the spell ends, when you take the Attack action on your turn, you can replace one of the attacks with a spell attack from the spell.

# Sanguine Vambraces
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*  
These red bracers ripple faintly with a bloody shimmer. While wearing them, you can use an action to expend 2 Hit Dice to cast the vampiric touch spell from them (spell attack bonus +7). Alternatively, you can forgo making an attack as part of the Attack action to cast this version of the spell or make subsequent spell attacks with it. You can still only make one attack with the spell on each of your turns.  
***Curse.*** These bracers are cursed, and attuning to them extends that curse to you. You remain cursed in this way until you're targeted by the remove curse spell or similar magic. While cursed, you gain the following flaw: "The feeling of siphoning life with necromancy is euphoric—so much so that I feel compelled to do it every day. I mustn't let others find out."
