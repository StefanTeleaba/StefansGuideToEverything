*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Steadfast Boots
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*  
Steadfast boots help keep you on your feet. If an effect moves you against your will along the ground, you can reduce the distance you are moved by up to 10 feet.  
While you're holding a weapon that has the Two-Handed property, you can take a Reaction to make one melee attack against a creature that enters the reach you have with that weapon.

---

*These thick, heavy boots are reinforced with iron along the sole.*
