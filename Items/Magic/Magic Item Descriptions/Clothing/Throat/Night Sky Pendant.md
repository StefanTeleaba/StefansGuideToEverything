*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Night Sky Pendant
*Wondrous Item, Artifact (Requires Attunement)*

***Diamond Skin.*** As a Bonus Action, you can cause black crystaline scales to envelop your body for 10 minutes.  
While the scales cover your body, your base Armor Class equals 10 plus your Dexterity and Constitution modifiers and you have Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage. In addition, spells of Level 1 or lower have no effect on you.  
You can use Diamond Skin twice. You regain one expended use when you finish a Short Rest, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
