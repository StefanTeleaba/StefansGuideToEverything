*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Mental Marbles
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*  
You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed, Frightened, Incapacitated and Stunned conditions.

*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed, Frightened, Incapacitated and Stunned conditions and you can remove one of the conditions listed above from yourself at the end of each of your turns.

*Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
You have Immunity to the Charmed, Frightened, Incapacitated and Stunned conditions.

# Mental Marbles
*Wondrous item, uncommon*  
This necklace is made of swirling beads. If you become charmed, frightened, incapacitated (but not unconscious), or stunned while wearing this necklace, it breaks, and that condition immediately ends on you. The necklace ceases to be magical once it breaks, and the beads scatter in all directions.
