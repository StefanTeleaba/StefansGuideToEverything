*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Phase Spider Cloak
*Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
This fine garment is made of blue, gray and white silk interwoven with faint, silvery threads and the hood is embroidered with eight silvery-white eyes. While wearing it, you gain the following benefits.  
***Ethereal Jaunt.*** As a Bonus Action, you can teleport from the Material Plane to the Ethereal Plane or vice versa.  
***Ethereal Sight.*** You can see 60 feet into the Ethereal Plane while on the Material Plane and vice versa.
