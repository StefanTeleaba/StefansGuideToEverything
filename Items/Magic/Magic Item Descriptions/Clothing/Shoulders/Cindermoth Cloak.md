*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Cindermoth Cloak
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*  
If a creature hits you with a melee attack roll, the creature starts Burning.

*Wondrous Item, Rare*  
You have Resistance to Fire damage while wearing this cloak. In addition, if a creature hits you with a melee attack roll, the creature takes 1d8 Fire damage and starts Burning.

*Wondrous Item, Very Rare*  
You have Immunity to Fire damage while wearing this cloak. In addition, if a creature hits you with a melee attack roll, the creature takes 2d8 Fire damage and starts Burning.
