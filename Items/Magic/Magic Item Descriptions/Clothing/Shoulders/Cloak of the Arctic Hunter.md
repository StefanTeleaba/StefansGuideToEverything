# Cloak of the Arctic Hunter
*Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
While you are attuned to this magic cloak, you have Resistance to Cold damage, you can tolerate temperatures of 0 degrees Fahrenheit or lower without any additional protection, and your armor does not impose Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks. You have Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks while on ice or snow, and you ignore Difficult Terrain created by ice or snow. In addition, if you make an attack roll while hidden and the roll misses, making the attack roll doesn't reveal your location.

# Cloak of the Arctic Hunter
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*  
This silvery cloak resembles a snowy owl. While wearing it, you have resistance to cold damage, and armor you're wearing doesn't impose disadvantage on your Dexterity (Stealth) checks if it normally would. In addition, you have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks while in snow, and difficult terrain composed of ice or snow doesn't cost you extra movement.  
Furthermore, your ranged attacks with a bow, crossbow, or thrown weapon are silent if you're hidden while wearing this cloak. As a result, missing with such an attack doesn't reveal your location.  
*If you feel like you're being watched, it is probably because you are prey.*
