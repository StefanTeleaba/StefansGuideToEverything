# Fey Wanderer's Cloak
*Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
While you are attuned to this magic cloak, you can cast the *Misty Step* spell without expending a spell slot. Creatures within 5 feet of the space you leave or appear at (your choice) must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC 15) or have the Charmed condition until the end of your next turn or until you or your allies damage it. You also know the locaitons of any portal to the Feywild within 3 miles of you.

# Fey Wanderer's Cloak
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*  
Planar energy from the Feywild has imbued this cloak with latent trans-dimensional magic. While wearing it, you can magically sense the direction to any portal to the Feywild within 1 mile of you. The cloak has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn, or all expended charges if you are a fey, have the fey ancestry trait, or are in the Feywild. While wearing the cloak, you can expend 1 of its charges to cast the misty step spell. When you do, you can choose to cause flowers to grow from the ground and bloom in any space within 5 feet of where you leave or appear as part of the spell (your choice each time). When you do, other creatures within those spaces must then succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by you until the end of your next turn or until you or your companions do anything harmful to it.
