*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Gauntlets of Flaming Fury
*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
When you take the Attack action, you can cause any melee weapon you are holding to be enveloped in flames. While the weapons are ablaze, they deal an extra 1d6 Fire damage on a hit. The flames last until you drop, stow, or sheathe the weapon. This property can't be used again until the next dawn.

*Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
When you take the Attack action, you can cause any melee weapon you are holding to be enveloped in flames. While the weapons are ablaze, they deal an extra 1d6 Fire damage on a hit. The flames last until you drop, stow, or sheathe the weapon.

# Gauntlets of Flaming Fury
*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)*  
While you wear both of these steel gauntlets, any non-magical weapon you grasp with either gauntlet is treated as a magic weapon. As a bonus action, you can use the gauntlets to cause magical flames to envelop one or two melee weapons in your grasp. Each flaming weapon deals an extra 1d6 fire damage on a hit. The flames last until you sheath or let go of either weapon. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.
