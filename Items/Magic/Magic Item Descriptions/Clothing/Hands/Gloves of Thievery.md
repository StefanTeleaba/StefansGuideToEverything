*Encyclopedia Magica Volume II 2e p.545*  
# Gloves of Thievery
**XP Value:** 1,000  
**GP Value:** 5,000  
*Unearthed Arcana*  
This seemingly normal handwear radiates a faint aura of enchantment and alteration. When placed snugly upon the hands, these gloves cannot be seen, except by means of magical sight or detection. Wearing them allows the possessor to open locks as a 7th-level thief and pick pockets as a 5th-level thief. If actually worn by a thief, the gloves enable him or her to gain 5% in both categories if already equal to or above the stated levels due to advancement. Otherwise, they operate the same for a thief as for any other wearer.
