*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Enspelled Wand
*Wand, Rarity Varies (Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)*  
Bound into this wand is a spell of level 8 or lower. The spell is determined when the wand is created and can be of any school of magic. The wand has 6 charges and regains 1d6 expended charges daily at dawn. While holding the wand, you can expend 1 charge to cast its spell. If you expend the wand’s last charge, roll 1d20. On a 1, the wand loses its properties and becomes a nonmagical wand.  
The level of the spell bound into the wand determines the spell’s saving throw DC and attack bonus, as well as the wand’s rarity, as shown in the following table.
| Spell Level | Rarity    | Save DC | Attack Bonus |
|:-----------:|:---------:|:-------:|:------------:|
| Cantrip     | Uncommon  | 13      | +5           |
| 1           | Uncommon  | 13      | +5           |
| 2           | Rare      | 13      | +5           |
| 3           | Rare      | 15      | +7           |
| 4           | Very Rare | 15      | +7           |
| 5           | Very Rare | 17      | +9           |
| 6           | Legendary | 17      | +9           |
| 7           | Legendary | 18      | +10          |
| 8           | Legendary | 18      | +10          |
