*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Sentinel Shield
*Shield (Shield), Uncommon*  
While holding this Shield, you have Advantage on Initiative rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks. The Shield is emblazoned with a symbol of an eye.

*Shield (Shield), Rare*  
You gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class while you wield this Shield. This bonus is in addition to the Shield's normal bonus to AC. While holding it, you have Advantage on Initiative rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks. The Shield is emblazoned with a symbol of an eye.

*Shield (Shield), Very Rare*  
You gain a +2 bonus to Armor Class while you wield this Shield. This bonus is in addition to the Shield's normal bonus to AC. While holding it, you have Advantage on Initiative rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks. The Shield is emblazoned with a symbol of an eye.
