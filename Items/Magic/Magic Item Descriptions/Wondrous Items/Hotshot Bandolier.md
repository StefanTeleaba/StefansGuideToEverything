# Hotshot Bandolier
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*  
***Quick Draw.*** Immediately after you roll Initiative, you can take a Reaction to make one ranged attack with a weapon. You can draw one weapon as part of this Reaction.  
***Exploding Bullets.*** This bandolier holds 4 *Exploding Firearm Bullets*. When you hit a creature with one of these bullets, the target of the attack and each creature within 5 feet of it make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 13), taking 2d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. The bandolier regains all expended bullets daily at dawn.

# Hotshot Bandolier
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*  
While wearing this magic bandolier, you can use your reaction when you roll initiative to make a single attack with a ranged weapon you're holding against a target within the weapon's normal range. You make the roll with disadvantage. If you're not already holding a ranged weapon, you can cause one to magically teleport to your open hand or hands as part of this reaction, provided the weapon is on your person.  
The bandolier produces its own +1 firearm bullets. These bullets are special: a target that's hit with one takes an extra 2d6 fire damage, and any other creature within 5 feet of the target must also succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take the same amount of fire damage. A removed bullet disappears in a small, crackling plume of smoke (like a firecracker) if it's not fired before the end of your next turn. 1d4 missing bullets reappear in the bandolier daily at dawn, up to maximum of 4.  
*Seems to me, when facing down a whole crew of bandits, speed and distraction are your greatest allies. Draw steel fast and lay 'em low: I guarantee when those explosive tips hit 'em in the chest, they'll lose their nerve right quick.*  
*Of course, I'm always quicker.*
