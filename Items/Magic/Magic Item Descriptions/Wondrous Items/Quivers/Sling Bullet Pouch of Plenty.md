*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Sling Bullet Pouch of Plenty
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*  
This pouch produces an endless supply of sling bullets. The pouch always holds 20 *Sling Bullets*. Another sling bullet is created immediately after you draw one from the pouch. A sling bullet created by the pouch vanishes the instant after it hits or misses a target.

*Wondrous Item, Rare*  
This pouch produces an endless supply of sling bullets. The pouch always holds 20 *+1 Sling Bullets*. Another sling bullet is created immediately after you draw one from the pouch. A sling bullet created by the pouch vanishes the instant after it hits or misses a target.

*Wondrous Item, Very Rare*  
This pouch produces an endless supply of sling bullets. The pouch always holds 20 *+2 Sling Bullets*. Another sling bullet is created immediately after you draw one from the pouch. A sling bullet created by the pouch vanishes the instant after it hits or misses a target.

---

A Sling Bullet Pouch of Plenty could be enchanted to supply different types of sling bullets: *Sling Bullets*, *+1 Sling Bullets*, *Acid Sling Bullets*, *Ranging Sling Bullets*, etc.
