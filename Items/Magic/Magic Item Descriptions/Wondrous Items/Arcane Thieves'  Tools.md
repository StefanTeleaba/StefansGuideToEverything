*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Arcane Thieves' Tools
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*  
You gain a +1 bonus to Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) checks made to pick a lock or disarm a trap with Thieves' Tools.  
***Arcane Thief.*** When you fail a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check to pick a lock or disarm a trap with Thieves' Tools, you can expend a spell slot as a Reaction to add a bonus to the roll, potentially causing it to succeed. The bonus equals your Intelligence modifier (minimum of +1) plus twice the level of the expended spell slot.

*Wondrous Item, Rare*  
You gain a +2 bonus to Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) checks made to pick a lock or disarm a trap with Thieves' Tools.  
***Arcane Thief.*** When you fail a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check to pick a lock or disarm a trap with Thieves' Tools, you can expend a spell slot as a Reaction to add a bonus to the roll, potentially causing it to succeed. The bonus equals your Intelligence modifier (minimum of +1) plus twice the level of the expended spell slot.  
***Spell.*** While holding the tools, you can use an action to cast *Knock*. The tools can't cast this spell again until the next dawn.

*Wondrous Item, Very Rare*  
You gain a +3 bonus to Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) checks made to pick a lock or disarm a trap with Thieves' Tools.  
***Arcane Thief.*** When you fail a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check to pick a lock or disarm a trap with Thieves' Tools, you can expend a spell slot as a Reaction to add a bonus to the roll, potentially causing it to succeed. The bonus equals your Intelligence modifier (minimum of +1) plus twice the level of the expended spell slot.  
***Spells.*** The tools have 10 charges and regain 1d6+4 charges daily at dawn. While holding them, you can expend charges to cast one of the following spells: *Arcane Lock* (2 charges), *Dispel Magic* (3 charges), *Knock* (2 charges).

*Magic Item Compendium 3.5e*
# Arcane Thieves' Tools
**Price (Item Level):** 1,400 gp (5th)  
**Body Slot:** — (held)  
**Caster Level:** 5th  
**Aura:** Faint; (DC 17) transmutation  
**Activation:** Swift (mental)  
**Weight:** 1 lb.  
*This set of picks, pins, and levers is housed in a supple leather bag decorated with arcane runes.*  
This set of masterwork thieves’ tools is the perfect accessory if you depend on a blend of magic and skill to achieve your thieving goals. In addition to granting the normal +2 circumstance bonus on Disable Device and Open Lock checks, these tools can grant a character with the trapfinding class feature a special benefit. When activating the tools, you can expend an arcane spell or arcane spell slot of 1st level or higher to gain a +5 competence bonus on either a Disable Device check or an Open Lock check begun before the end of that turn.  
*Prerequisites:* Craft Wondrous Item, knock, trapfinding.  
*Cost to Create:* 700 gp, 56 XP, 2 days.

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*  
***Masterwork Tool.*** You gain a +1 bonus to Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) checks made to pick a lock or disarm a trap with Thieves' Tools.  
***Arcane Tool.*** When activating the tools, you can expend a spell slot of 1st-level or higher to gain a +5 bonus to one Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) checks and Dexterity checks made to pick locks until the end of your turn.
