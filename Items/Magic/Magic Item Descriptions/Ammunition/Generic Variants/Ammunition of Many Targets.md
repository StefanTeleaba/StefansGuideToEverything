*Homebrew 5.5e*
# Ammunition of Many Targets
*Weapon (Any Ammunition), Rare* 
This piece of ammunition can leap to as many as three other targets of your choice, each of which must be within your weapon's short range and within 30 feet of eachother. You make a separate attack roll against each target.
